Privacy Policy

SaldoSehat is a local-first personal finance application developed by Max-Q. It helps users record and review income, expenses, wallets, savings targets, budgets, loans, receivables, reports, exports, and related personal finance data.

Most SaldoSehat data is stored locally on your device. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled by the app, how advertising and purchases work, and what choices are available to you.

1. Information you enter

SaldoSehat may store information that you enter into the app, including transaction amounts, dates, wallet names, categories, custom categories, savings targets, budget information, loan and receivable notes, due dates, and other notes that you create.

This information is intended for your own personal finance records. SaldoSehat does not require you to create an online account to use its main local features.

2. Local storage and on-device processing

Main app data is stored locally on your device. Local records may include your finance entries, preferences, notification settings, hidden feature-usage credits, and local records needed to maintain Premium access status.

If the app is uninstalled, app data is cleared, or the device is lost or damaged, locally stored data may be lost unless you created and retained a separate export or backup. Device backups may behave differently depending on your operating-system and device settings.

3. Exports and backups

SaldoSehat may allow you to export data, including to Excel-compatible files, and may provide backup and restore features. SaldoSehat backup files use the .saldosehat format.

After an export or backup file is created outside the app, you are responsible for protecting it, choosing where it is stored, and deciding whether to share it. Do not share exports, backups, or screenshots that contain sensitive financial information with anyone you do not trust.

4. Advertising and Google AdMob

The free version of SaldoSehat may display banner, interstitial, native, rewarded, and app-open advertisements through Google AdMob and the Google Mobile Ads SDK, subject to applicable consent and availability.

Google advertising services may process technical and advertising-related information, such as device or app identifiers, IP address (which may be used to estimate your general location), device and operating-system information, advertising interactions, and diagnostics. This information is used for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention, depending on your region, device settings, and privacy choices.

SaldoSehat is designed not to send your transaction amounts, wallet names, category names, loan notes, receivable notes, reports, exported files, backup files, or other personal finance records to AdMob for advertising purposes.

5. Advertising consent and privacy choices

Where required, SaldoSehat uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request or record advertising privacy choices before requesting advertisements.

Users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland may be asked to consent to advertising purposes or manage their choices. Users in supported United States jurisdictions may be offered applicable opt-out choices.

When required by the advertising privacy system, SaldoSehat will provide a Privacy Options entry in the app so you can review or change available advertising choices. Declining personalized advertising does not prevent access to the app's main local finance features. Ads may still be shown in a form allowed by your choices and applicable requirements.

6. Rewarded advertisements and limited feature usage

Free users may voluntarily watch a rewarded advertisement to unlock five additional uses of selected features. The internal usage balance does not reset daily and is not displayed as a numerical balance to the user.

One usage is deducted only after one of the following actions completes successfully:

  • using a Custom Category for income;
  • using a Custom Category for expenses;
  • applying a Custom Date Range on the Home screen; or
  • successfully exporting data to an Excel-compatible file.

No usage is deducted when an action is cancelled or fails. Rewarded usage is added only after the advertising SDK confirms that the reward has been earned. Local usage records may include an internal balance and event history used to prevent duplicate rewards or duplicate deductions.

Local rewarded usage credits may be lost if the app is uninstalled, app data is cleared, or the device is replaced.

7. Premium Lifetime and Google Play Billing

SaldoSehat may offer an optional one-time Premium Lifetime purchase through Google Play Billing. Premium Lifetime is intended to remove ads and provide unlimited access to Custom Category features, Custom Date Range, and Excel export while the entitlement is active.

Google Play processes the payment transaction. SaldoSehat may receive and store information required to determine purchase status and restore Premium access, such as the product identifier, purchase state, acknowledgement status, purchase time, and a purchase-token reference or hash.

Max-Q does not receive or store your full payment-card details. Payment information is handled by Google Play according to Google's own terms and privacy practices. A valid Premium purchase may be restored through Google Play when you use the same eligible Google account.

8. Notifications

SaldoSehat may use local notifications for activity reminders, due-date reminders, savings-target reminders, or similar app functions. You can manage notification permissions through your device settings and may be able to use more private notification wording through the app settings.

When notification details are hidden, SaldoSehat uses generic reminder text. When details are not hidden, reminders may be more informative, but the app should avoid displaying money amounts in notification titles or bodies.

9. Ask

Ask is an optional online AI feature. When you choose to enable it, the app sends your question, relevant recent conversation, and verified financial information needed for your request through Firebase to an external AI provider (OpenAI). Only the context selected for the request is sent; your entire local finance database is not uploaded. Ask responses may be incomplete or inaccurate and are not professional financial advice.

Financial facts prepared by the app are limited to the relevant numeric summaries; original wallet and savings-target labels and raw transaction rows are excluded from that fact context. Your question and conversation text can still contain personal or financial details that you type. Do not include passwords, payment-card details, or information unrelated to your request.

When online Ask needs backend access and no Firebase identity is active, the app creates an anonymous Firebase authentication identity. This is not an email/password sign-in account. Firebase and Google Cloud process authentication, app-integrity, network, and operational information to provide the service and prevent abuse. The backend maintains limited rate-limit, usage, cost, and diagnostic metadata.

Using online Ask requires a separate optional permission in the app, after accepting the Privacy Policy and Terms. You can change that choice in Privacy settings. Declining keeps local finance features available. Ask cannot save transactions without your explicit confirmation. Withdrawing permission prevents new Ask requests, but cannot withdraw information already processed by external services.

10. Third-party services

Depending on the version and features enabled, SaldoSehat may use:

  • Google AdMob and Google Mobile Ads services for advertisements;
  • Google User Messaging Platform for advertising privacy choices;
  • Google Play Billing for the Premium Lifetime purchase;
  • Firebase Authentication, App Check, and Cloud Functions for authenticated online Ask;
  • OpenAI for processing online Ask requests and generating responses.

Max-Q uses Firebase, Google Cloud, and OpenAI to provide online Ask. Google also processes advertising and payment information under its applicable policies and terms. Max-Q remains responsible for its own handling of information. Provider practices are described in the Google Privacy Policy, Firebase privacy information, and OpenAI Privacy Policy.

11. Data retention and deletion

Main finance data remains on your device until you delete it, clear the app's data, or uninstall SaldoSehat. Available in-app deletion controls can be used to remove individual records or other supported data.

Exported or backed-up files stored outside the app are not automatically deleted when the app is uninstalled. You are responsible for deleting copies stored in device folders, external storage, cloud storage, or other third-party services.

Advertising choices and purchase records handled by Google may be retained according to Google's policies and applicable legal requirements. Information sent through online Ask may be processed and retained according to the relevant service providers' policies; deleting local app data or withdrawing Ask permission does not automatically delete data that has already been processed externally.

Anonymous authentication identities and operational rate-limit or cost records can remain after an Ask request ends. These records are not all subject to automatic deletion. Withdrawing Ask permission does not itself delete them. Service logs and provider records have separate retention rules.

For questions or requests concerning information processed online, contact saldosehat.support@maxqstudio.dev. We may need reasonable information to identify the relevant service records. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or a complete finance backup. We do not promise that clearing local data deletes external records or that all providers use a particular retention period.

12. Security

We aim to keep SaldoSehat simple and safe to use, but no storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for protecting your device, app access, export files, backup files, screenshots, Google account, and any copies of your data.

13. Intended audience and children

SaldoSehat is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly ask children to submit personal financial information through Ask.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to features, third-party services, security practices, legal requirements, or platform policies. The "Last updated" date will be revised when material changes are published.
