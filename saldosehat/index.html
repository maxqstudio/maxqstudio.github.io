About SaldoSehat

Clear records for everyday personal finance

SaldoSehat is designed to help you record and review personal cash flow through a simple, local-first experience. It is not a bank, lender, pay-later service, credit-scoring service, or official financial advisor.

Daily records

Record income and expenses with dates, categories, wallets, and notes so your cash flow is easier to review.

Wallets

Separate balances across cash, bank accounts, e-wallets, and prepaid e-money cards.

Targets and budgets

Create savings targets or budget plans and review your progress over time.

Loans and receivables

Record money you need to pay or receive, including notes, status, and due dates when needed.

Export and backup

Create personal exports and backup files, then store them in a secure location you trust.

Free with ads

The free version may show banner and app-open ads and offer optional rewarded ads for selected Custom and Export features.

Calendar and reminders

Review financial dates and upcoming obligations, with reminders you can configure in the app.

Your view, your preferences

Choose your language, theme, number and currency display. Hide balances whenever you need more privacy on screen.

Custom categories

Organize records with built-in categories or categories that fit the way you manage money.

Premium Lifetime

An optional one-time purchase removes ads and unlocks unlimited Custom features and Excel export.
