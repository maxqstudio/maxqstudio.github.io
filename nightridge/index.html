Night Ridge

Your phone becomes a signal light for night rides, trail hikes and camp. Light the whole screen, or blink the rear camera flash.

Coming soon on Google Play Support

What it does

Screen light

The screen turns into a bright panel in landscape, at full brightness, and goes back to normal when you stop.

  • 15 patterns: Steady, Slow blink, Double pulse, Breathing, Running bars, Aurora, Running text, Heartbeat, Triple pulse, SOS, Wave, Alternating, Scanner, Comet and Custom.
  • The Stop button hides after a few seconds. Tap the screen to bring it back, or press Back.
  • Running text and Custom start after one short video per session.

Rear LED

Blink the camera flash in six rhythms, with pause, next and stop controls in the notification.

Premium, one-time

Any color with RGB or HEX, RGB cycle and rainbow, 10 text fonts, and no banner or app-open ads.

Night Ridge is a backup visibility aid. It does not replace a dedicated bike light or headlamp. Flashing patterns may affect people with photosensitive conditions.
