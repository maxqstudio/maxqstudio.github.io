2. What Night Ridge is, and is not

Night Ridge is a supplementary visibility aid. It does not replace a proper bike light, headlamp, reflective gear or any lighting required by law where you ride or walk. A phone screen or camera flash may not be visible to everyone, in every direction, or in every condition. You remain responsible for your own safety and for following traffic and trail rules.

Mount or hold your phone only where it is secure and does not distract you. Do not operate the app while riding or driving.

3. Flashing light warning

Several patterns flash or flicker. Flashing light can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy and can dazzle others. Stop using flashing patterns if you or anyone nearby feels unwell, and do not point the light at drivers' eyes.

4. Battery and heat

Full-brightness screen use and the camera flash drain the battery and can warm the device. Keep enough charge for calls and navigation. Stop if the device becomes hot.

5. Free use, ads and rewarded sessions

Night Ridge is free with ads. Running text and Custom unlock for one session after you finish one rewarded video; stopping the light, pressing Back or leaving the app ends that session. If no video is available, those modes cannot start. You must not manipulate ads or rewards.
