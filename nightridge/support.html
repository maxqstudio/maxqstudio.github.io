Questions

How do I stop the screen light?

Tap the screen to show Stop, or press Back. Leaving the app also stops it.

The rear LED does not turn on.

Allow the camera permission. Close other apps that use the camera or flashlight, then try again. Some devices have no compatible rear flash.

Why do Running text and Custom need a video?

Each session of these two modes starts after one rewarded video, also with Premium. The other 13 patterns start right away.

How do I restore Premium on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Google account, open the Premium tab and tap Restore purchase.

Can I change the language?

Night Ridge follows your phone's language. On Android 13 and later you can pick a language just for Night Ridge in Settings, Apps, Night Ridge, Language.
