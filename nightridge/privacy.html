Privacy Policy

Night Ridge for Android (com.maxq.nightridge) · Effective November 27, 2026

In short

Night Ridge has no account and no server of its own. Your settings, waypoints, trails, routes and voyages stay on your device. Your location is used on the device only and is never sent to Max-Q Studio. The camera permission only switches the flash on and off; Night Ridge never takes photos or video. The free version shows Google AdMob ads, which collect data as described below.

1. Who we are

Night Ridge is published by Max-Q Studio. Contact: support@maxqstudio.dev.

2. Data stored on your device

Night Ridge saves these settings locally in the app's private storage: theme, selected light source and pattern, your running-text message, chosen color and font, Custom animation timing, whether you finished the walkthrough, a cached Premium status, and a count of light sessions used to decide when to ask for a review. If you use the outdoor tools, it also saves the waypoints, ports, recorded trails, saved routes and voyage logs you create. This data is not sent to Max-Q Studio. Device backup of app data is turned off. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: needed by Android to control the rear flash LED. Night Ridge does not open the camera preview, take pictures, or record video.
  • Notifications: used for the ongoing notification with Pause, Next and Stop while the rear LED runs. If you deny it, the flash still works.
  • Location (precise or approximate): used by Waypoints & Trails, Navigate, Marine Mode, sunrise and sunset times, and the Tools tab to show your position, speed, course and distance. You can allow approximate location only; some features are then less accurate. Night Ridge does not request background location. A trail or voyage keeps recording with the screen off only while a visible notification shows that recording is running (foreground service).
  • Internet and network state: used to load map tiles, and by Google AdMob, Google's consent tools and Google Play Billing.
  • Wake lock and foreground service: keep the rear LED pattern, or a trail or voyage recording you started, running while the screen is off.

Sensors such as the compass, barometer, humidity and temperature sensors need no permission and their readings stay on your device. Night Ridge does not request contacts, storage, microphone or account access.

3a. Maps

Maps are loaded from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org), using OpenStreetMap data. To deliver map tiles, that server receives your IP address and the area of the map being shown, but not your GPS position or your saved places. Tiles are cached on your device so areas you already opened can be shown offline. If you choose to open a waypoint in Google Maps, Google Maps receives that waypoint's coordinates.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a banner ad and may show an app-open ad when you return to the app. Running text and Custom start after you choose to watch a rewarded video; Night Ridge only uses the reward result to unlock one session. No ad is shown over an active light.

Google Mobile Ads may collect or process your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), the advertising ID or app set ID, ad interactions, and diagnostic information, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in ads.

5. Consent and privacy choices

Night Ridge uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for advertising consent where the law requires it. When required, a Privacy choices entry appears in the Premium tab so you can change your choice. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

6. Premium purchase (Google Play)

Premium is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. Night Ridge receives the purchase status from Google Play on your device to unlock Premium and caches that status locally. Night Ridge does not receive your payment card details. Google handles payment data under its own terms.

7. Reviews

After a few light sessions, Night Ridge may show Google Play's in-app review prompt. Google decides whether it appears. Your rating and review go to Google Play; Night Ridge does not see them.

8. Children

Night Ridge is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Security

Network traffic from Google's SDKs uses encrypted connections. Night Ridge stores no personal data on any Max-Q Studio server.

10. Changes

If Night Ridge's data practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy: support@maxqstudio.dev.
