Privacy Policy

Night Ridge for Android (com.maxq.nightridge) · Effective November 12, 2026

In short

Night Ridge has no account and no server of its own. Your settings stay on your device. The camera permission only switches the flash on and off; Night Ridge never takes photos or video. The free version shows Google AdMob ads, which collect data as described below.

1. Who we are

Night Ridge is published by Max-Q Studio. Contact: support@maxqstudio.dev.

2. Data stored on your device

Night Ridge saves these settings locally in the app's private storage: theme, selected light source and pattern, your running-text message, chosen color and font, Custom animation timing, whether you finished the walkthrough, a cached Premium status, and a count of light sessions used to decide when to ask for a review. This data is not sent to Max-Q Studio. Device backup of app data is turned off. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: needed by Android to control the rear flash LED. Night Ridge does not open the camera preview, take pictures, or record video.
  • Notifications: used for the ongoing notification with Pause, Next and Stop while the rear LED runs. If you deny it, the flash still works.
  • Internet and network state: used by Google AdMob, Google's consent tools and Google Play Billing.
  • Wake lock and foreground service: keep the rear LED pattern running while the screen is off.

Night Ridge does not request location, contacts, storage, microphone or account access.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a banner ad and may show an app-open ad when you return to the app. Running text and Custom start after you choose to watch a rewarded video; Night Ridge only uses the reward result to unlock one session. No ad is shown over an active light.

Google Mobile Ads may collect or process your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), the advertising ID or app set ID, ad interactions, and diagnostic information, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in ads.

5. Consent and privacy choices

Night Ridge uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for advertising consent where the law requires it. When required, a Privacy choices entry appears in the Premium tab so you can change your choice. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

6. Premium purchase (Google Play)

Premium is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. Night Ridge receives the purchase status from Google Play on your device to unlock Premium and caches that status locally. Night Ridge does not receive your payment card details. Google handles payment data under its own terms.

7. Reviews

After a few light sessions, Night Ridge may show Google Play's in-app review prompt. Google decides whether it appears. Your rating and review go to Google Play; Night Ridge does not see them.

8. Children

Night Ridge is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Security

Network traffic from Google's SDKs uses encrypted connections. Night Ridge stores no personal data on any Max-Q Studio server.

10. Changes

If Night Ridge's data practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy: support@maxqstudio.dev.
