About Blend In!

Hide like a chameleon. Seek like a detective.

Hiders are tiny and pale white, and slowly take on the colour of whatever is around them. The shooter has one paint pistol, a whistle, and the security cameras. Who finds whom?

Become anything

Copy the furniture, decorations and toys around you, hang on a wall as a picture, or let your body melt into the colour behind you.

Hide inside

Get into wardrobes, drawers, the fridge, chests and boxes, slip between books, sink into plants or crawl under a rug, then move about inside.

Hunt with a paint pistol

Whistle and listen for the answers, crawl to look under tables, open furniture and watch the other rooms on CCTV.

Story mode, Season 1

The Chameleon Powder Case: ten chapters, each opening one more room of the mansion. Play as Detective Rama, then as Lala of the Shadow Gang.

Wi-Fi multiplayer

Play with friends on the same Wi-Fi, up to 10 players. The host chooses how many shooters and fills empty places with bots.

Looks, not advantages

Outfits, weapons and paint colours with their own splashes. Coins only buy looks, never an advantage in a round.
