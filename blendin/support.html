Frequently asked questions

How do I play with friends?

Connect every phone to the same Wi-Fi. One player opens Multiplayer, Over Wi-Fi, Create room; the others open the same screen and join the room. The host picks the number of players (up to 10), shooters, bots and hide time. Online play over the internet is coming later.

My friends can't see my room.

Make sure all phones are on the same Wi-Fi network (not mobile data or a guest network that isolates devices). You can also join by typing the host's IP address, shown at the top of the room.

Why does the multiplayer house have fewer rooms?

Rooms open as you finish story chapters. In multiplayer the house uses the rooms the host has opened.

Do I need an account?

No. As a guest your progress stays on your phone. Sign in with Google to keep it safe in the cloud, continue on another phone, and to buy coins or Premium.

"Account in use on another device"

Only one phone saves to your account at a time, so progress never clashes. Tap "Use it on this phone" to continue here; the game first loads the latest save from your account.

I bought coins but didn't get them.

Make sure you are signed in with the same account and open the game while online; pending purchases are delivered automatically. If they still don't arrive, email us with your Google Play order ID (it starts with GPA.).

How do I cancel Premium?

Open Google Play, Profile, Payments & subscriptions, Subscriptions, Blend In!, Cancel. Premium stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I delete my account?

See Delete your account. You can do it in the game or by email.

The game is slow on my phone.

Open Settings, Graphics and choose Low or Auto Smooth. Lower graphics turn off real-time shadows and some lights.
