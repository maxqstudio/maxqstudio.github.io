About Pretio

Make everyday price comparisons easier

Record the prices you find, check your saved price history, compare products fairly, and plan what to buy next. AI helps suggest product identities, but it does not replace checking the package and seller.

Your own prices, available when you need them.

Save products, stores, prices, units, and shopping-list items on your device. Best Price is based on your recorded price history, not a guaranteed live store offer.

Two scan choices: Barcode and Product photo.

A Product photo can return an AI-suggested identity without requiring a barcode. Review the suggestion before adding a product or a shopping-list item. Product Insight is available from Product Detail when supported.

Features

Find, compare, and remember prices

Barcode & photo scan

Choose a barcode scan or photograph a product. AI-generated product suggestions may be incomplete; confirm the identity before saving.

Price history

Keep a record of prices by product and store, and revisit your saved best price over time.

Compare by unit

Compare quantities and unit prices to find better value among similar products.

Compare by category

Review category rankings and compare options using the prices you recorded.

Shopping list

Build a shopping list, review quantities and estimated totals, and add a product from Scan Result.

Online price search

Open online product listings for comparison. Prices and availability depend on external sellers and search results.

Store catalogs

Explore available store catalogs and promotions for supported locations.

Product Insight

Open AI-assisted product details when available from Product Detail. Treat generated descriptions and estimates as suggestions, not verified facts.

Local shopping records

Saved products, price history, stores, and shopping lists are kept locally on your device in Pretio 1.4.5+62. No Pretio account or cloud synchronization is needed to use these local features.

Optional online features

Photo-based product recognition can send the selected image to Google's Gemini service through Firebase AI Logic. Online search and catalogs may contact external services. AI suggestions and online prices can be inaccurate or unavailable.

Advertising and privacy

Google Mobile Ads and other third-party services may process technical information according to their policies. Pretio does not use your locally saved prices or shopping-list contents to target advertisements.
