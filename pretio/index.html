About Pretio

Made to help you compare prices more clearly

Pretio is designed for everyday shopping decisions. Save the prices you see, compare them later, and keep useful product notes without needing an account.

Pretio helps you save product prices, compare prices, and remember the best-value option.

It is useful when you want to remember a price, compare the same product from different stores, or decide which option gives the best value.

Your data stays on your device. No login is required.

That means your product records, saved prices, and shopping list stay under your control. You decide if you want to back them up or export them yourself.

Local Shopping Data

Your products, saved prices, stores, notes, and shopping list remain stored on your device. Location can be selected manually, inferred from your IP, or improved with optional foreground approximate device location.

Advertising and Privacy

Pretio may use Google Mobile Ads. Google may process device and advertising information according to your privacy choices and its own policies.

Your Control

Pretio does not send your product names, saved prices, stores, notes, shopping-list contents, backups, or exports as advertising-targeting data.
