About Pretio

Make everyday price comparisons easier

Record the prices you find, check your saved price history, compare products fairly, and plan what to buy next. AI helps suggest product identities, but it does not replace checking the package and seller.

Your own prices, available when you need them.

Save products, stores, prices, units, Watchlist items, and shopping-list items on your device, and optionally sign in with Google to sync them across your phones. Home Best Deal highlights your largest percentage savings against your historical average (falling back to your lowest recorded price), based on your own price history rather than a guaranteed live store offer.

Two scan choices: Barcode and Product photo.

A Product photo can return an AI-suggested identity without requiring a barcode. Review the suggestion before adding a product or a shopping-list item. Product Insight is available from Product Detail when supported.

What's new in 1.5.0

Best Deal, Product Watchlist, and reliable multi-provider AI

Home Best Deal

Home spotlights your Best Deal — the product with the largest percentage savings compared to your historical average price, falling back to your lowest recorded price.

Product Highlights & Watchlist

Home displays up to 5 Product Highlights plus a dedicated Product Highlights page with live search and one Filter & Sort button (Watchlist, Sort, Store, verified Area). Compare identical watched products directly against your price history in a single card and prefill Add to Price History or Shopping List.

Multi-provider AI fallback

Barcode AI, photo product identification, and Product Insight route through Pretio's backend with automatic fallback (Google Gemini primary, OpenAI and Anthropic fallback) for higher availability. Failed AI attempts are never counted against your quota.

Cloud sync & multiple devices

Optional Google sign-in backs up and syncs your products, prices, stores, and shopping lists across phones (1 active phone on Free, up to 3 on Premium), with Recently Deleted restore and 30-day account recovery.

Security hardening & app updates

Non-anonymous Google sign-in is required for cloud sync and AI, legacy pre-1.5.0 AI endpoints are retired, purchase verification is rate-limited, and an in-app update check notifies you when a newer version is available.

Country-scoped highlights & decimal prices

Product Highlights and store catalogs match your detected country and chosen province/city, while decimal pricing gives exact unit comparisons.

Your data is never auto-deleted

If you go over a limit (for example after a plan ends), Pretio never deletes your data automatically. New items are blocked until you delete some or upgrade; everything you already saved stays.

Deleting your data

Delete All Data removes your records on the device and, when signed in, in the cloud; you must type DELETE and wait a short countdown. Deleting your account can be undone within 30 days. Backups are manual, and Pretio asks if you want a backup first. See Data and privacy.

Local shopping records

Saved products, price history, stores, Watchlist items, and shopping lists are kept locally on your device. No Pretio account or cloud synchronization is needed to use these local features. If you choose to sign in with Google in Pretio 1.5.0, your core records are also synced to your account.

Optional online & AI features

AI product recognition, barcode enrichment, and Product Insight send requests through Pretio's backend to Google Gemini (primary) and, if unavailable, configured fallback AI providers (OpenAI and Anthropic). Grounded search results are never persistently cached by Pretio. Online search, Product Highlights, and catalogs may contact external services, and AI suggestions or online prices can be inaccurate or unavailable.

Advertising and privacy

Google Mobile Ads and other third-party services may process technical information according to their policies. Pretio does not use your locally saved prices or shopping-list contents to target advertisements.
