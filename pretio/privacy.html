Privacy & Data Safety

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2, 2026 · Last reviewed: November 2, 2026

Your shopping records remain local

Pretio stores your products, saved prices, shopping list, stores, notes, and related shopping records on your device. Pretio does not send the contents of those records to AdMob for ad targeting. When you deliberately use Compare Online or Where To Buy, only product information included in the visible Google Shopping search query is sent to Google as described below.

1. Local app data

Pretio does not require a Pretio account. Your core shopping data remains on your device unless you choose to use Compare Online, create a backup, export a file, restore a file, or otherwise share data using your device's system features. Compare Online is optional, internet-dependent, and does not run until you select the Online action for a product.

2. Backup, export, restore, and deletion

You can back up Pretio data, export saved product and price-history data as CSV, and export a Shopping List receipt as PDF. A receipt PDF may include the store name you enter, shopping-list items, quantities, prices, subtotal, tax, discount, currency, date, and calculated total. Pretio writes these files only when you choose an export and select a destination through Android's file picker. Once a file leaves Pretio, the storage provider, destination app, or person receiving it may process that file under their own terms and privacy practices.

You can use Delete All Data to remove products, saved prices, your shopping list, stores, and related local records from the device. App preferences such as language, theme, number format, currency, and text size may be preserved as described in the app.

Delete All Data affects local app records only. It does not delete server-side records created by earlier versions of Pretio, including legacy shared Product Insight caches, or Google Play purchase-entitlement records stored by Pretio's backend. Pretio does not create a Pretio account and currently has no operational process for deleting server-side records on an individual request. The data and privacy information page explains what can and cannot be identified or removed. You may contact support with privacy questions, but an email does not mean server data has been deleted.

3. Compare Online and Where To Buy

When you select Online for a product, Pretio creates a Google Shopping search query using the current product name, the saved brand when available and not already represented in the product name, the saved package size when available, and the word “price.” That visible query is sent to Google over the internet, and the Google Shopping results page is displayed through an in-app web view.

Where To Buy is a separate, optional Google Shopping search opened from Scan Result. Depending on what is available after a Barcode or Product photo scan, the visible query may include a scanned barcode or an AI-suggested product name, brand, and package size. A photo scan does not require a barcode. Pretio may add the selected country, region, or city as search context. Google receives the visible query and displays its own results; Pretio does not extract or store prices or offers from that page.

Pretio does not include your saved price history, stores, notes, shopping list, backups, exports, or locally calculated Best Price in the Compare Online query. Best Price is calculated from your local Pretio records and is not obtained from Google.

Google may process the search query and technical information associated with the request, such as your IP address, device or browser information, cookies or similar technologies, account state when signed in, and interactions with Google Shopping or Google Search, according to Google's policies and your Google settings.

Google-owned shopping or search navigation may remain inside Pretio. When you select an external result, Android may open the destination in a browser, system chooser, website, or compatible marketplace app. That destination may process information under its own privacy policy. Pretio does not control the destination's data practices.

Pretio does not scrape search pages or write visited URLs, search-result content, online prices, or online offers into Pretio's product or price-history database. The web view, browser, Google, Android, or destination service may still retain technical browsing data such as cookies, cache, history, or interaction records under their own controls and policies.

4. Location and local catalog relevance

Pretio uses the country selected for your Google Play account to choose country-level catalog content, Product Highlights, and the supported location directory. Pretio obtains this country from Google Play Billing configuration. This is your Google Play country, not a measurement of your current physical location, and it may differ from the country where you are physically located.

Pretio resolves Google Play country for each app session and does not store the Billing configuration result as a persistent Country setting or future Country authority. For app functionality, Pretio may cache country-specific Location V2 directory content locally and persist country-scoped canonical identities for selected provinces, states, regions, cities, or municipalities. On a later launch, Pretio resolves Google Play country first and then validates saved subdivision identities against that country; cached directory content and saved subdivisions cannot establish Country. This information is not used to create or enhance a user profile or for advertising or marketing targeting. Country is not manually editable in Pretio.

You may select a province, state, region, city, or municipality where supported. If you explicitly tap Use approximate location and grant Android location permission, Android's coarse foreground location may resolve a country, province, and city. Pretio saves the resolved administrative area and its canonical identifiers in local app data and uses it as the active location in later sessions, even if its country differs from your Google Play country. It remains active until you select another approximate location or clear local app data. Pretio does not store raw device coordinates. Android and its geocoder service may process device location under their own policies. Pretio does not use precise or background location for this feature.

AI-assisted Barcode and Product photo scans (Pretio v1.4.4 and newer)

When you scan an unknown barcode, Pretio may request an AI-suggested product identity through Google Gemini using Firebase AI Logic. This user-triggered request may include the barcode, app language, selected country, and relevant locally saved product identity fields, such as the name, brand, size, and category when available. Pretio does not send your full shopping database, saved price history, stores, notes, or shopping list with the AI request.

When you choose Scan > Product and take or select a photo, Pretio compresses it on the device into JPEG (longest side up to 1,280 pixels; outgoing file no larger than 1 MiB) and removes EXIF and GPS metadata before sending the image to Google Gemini through Firebase AI Logic for product identification. The AI may optionally recognize a barcode from the same photo, but a barcode is not required; a recognizable product may be shown as an AI suggestion without one. Pretio does not save the selected photo to your product records; temporary camera or gallery files are removed on a best-effort basis. Google may process the submitted image and technical request data under its service terms. Photos are not sent to AdMob for advertising purposes by Pretio.

Product Insight report

In Pretio v1.4.4+61, Scan Result displays available AI Suggested product information, not a duplicate Product Insight report. The separate Product Insight feature remains available through Product Detail for supported products. When you request it, Pretio sends relevant barcode, country, response language, and applicable product identity context to Google Gemini using Firebase AI Logic. When a request does not produce useful content, Pretio may retry that Product Insight operation (up to five model attempts in total); this retry policy does not apply to Photo Product identification or Barcode Scan Enrichment. Descriptions, source links, product identity, and estimated nutrition can be incomplete or incorrect and must not be treated as verified safety, health, or nutritional advice.

AI storage, providers, and earlier versions

Pretio v1.4.4 does not persist AI-generated responses, Google Search grounding links, Search Suggestions, or Product Insight reports to its previous on-device Product Insight cache or saved product records. This does not mean that Google or other service providers retain nothing: Gemini API data handling depends on the chosen provider and service terms. When Google Search Grounding is used, Google may retain prompts, context, and responses for 30 days under its Gemini API terms. If Firebase AI monitoring is enabled, its logging and retention settings may also apply. Pretio does not claim zero retention by external providers.

Earlier Pretio versions used Firebase Cloud Functions, shared Product Insight caches, and request-activity or rate-limit records. Existing shared caches and Google Play purchase-entitlement records may remain on the legacy backend, and their expiry or freshness periods do not guarantee deletion. The current client-side Firebase AI Logic flow is different from that legacy product-report processing. Purchase verification and some other app features may still use Pretio's Firebase backend. No individual server-side deletion service has been established; see the data and privacy information page.

Pretio does not use a dedicated IP-geolocation service to determine your country and does not send a country-detection request to ipapi.co for this feature. Google Play and other network services may still receive ordinary technical network information, such as your IP address, under their own policies when your device communicates with them.

The supported location directory is bundled in Pretio for offline use. Catalog Promo and Product Highlight content is read from Pretio's Firebase public-content database when needed and cached on your device for 24 hours. Pretio uses the selected country, province, or city context to choose relevant content. Firebase receives ordinary technical request information when the app reads remote content. A supported location may have no available catalog or Product Highlights yet.

Pretio's supported language packs are bundled in the app. Pretio does not download language packs from GitHub or the Max-Q Studio website during normal app use.

For Compare Online, Pretio may use the current location context, such as country, province, city, language, or currency, to localize the visible search experience. Pretio does not need to send raw device latitude or longitude to Google Shopping for this purpose. A website opened inside or outside Pretio may have its own separate geolocation request and privacy practices.

5. Advertising

The free version of Pretio may use Google AdMob to display banner, native, app-open, transition interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. A locally stored counter starts with 10 remaining usage units and decreases on eligible transitions: Compare Item uses 1, Compare Category uses 1, Where To Buy uses 2, Compare Online uses 2, and a selected Catalog Promo page uses 1. After an interstitial is dismissed, the counter resets to 10. The counter remains on your device and is not sent to AdMob as product data. An interstitial is shown only when ad eligibility, consent, and ad availability permit. A rewarded advertisement is shown only after you choose to watch it, for example before an eligible saved-data export or Shopping List receipt export.

Google Mobile Ads technology may automatically collect or process information such as:

  • your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • app and advertisement interactions, such as app launches, taps, and video views;
  • diagnostic information about app and advertising-SDK performance; and
  • device or account identifiers, which may include the Android advertising ID or app set ID.

This information may be used by Google for advertising, analytics, security, and fraud-prevention purposes. The exact data handled can depend on the SDK version, your device settings, region, consent choices, and the ad-serving mode.

Learn more through Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising technologies information.

6. Consent and privacy choices

Pretio may use Google's User Messaging Platform to request, store, and update advertising privacy choices where required. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalized, non-personalized, limited, or unavailable.

When required, Pretio will provide a Privacy Options entry in the app so you can review or change available advertising choices. You can also manage or reset your Android advertising ID through your device settings, where supported.

7. Notifications and inactivity reminder

Pretio may ask for Android notification permission. If you grant it, Pretio can schedule a local inactivity reminder on your device approximately 72 hours after the app is opened, reminding you to review saved prices and plan your next shopping list. Opening Pretio again resets that reminder schedule.

The current inactivity reminder is scheduled locally on the device using Android notification functionality; Pretio does not use a Pretio account or remote push-messaging service to deliver this reminder. If you deny notification permission, the reminder is not scheduled and Pretio's core features remain available.

8. Rewarded export

For free users, Pretio may require a completed rewarded advertisement before an eligible export. Current eligible exports include saved product and price-history data as CSV and a Shopping List receipt as PDF. Pretio uses the rewarded-ad completion and dismissal result only to decide whether export authorization is granted.

Pretio does not send the contents of your exported CSV or PDF file to the advertising provider as part of this reward flow. This includes your products, saved prices, stores, notes, shopping-list entries, store name, tax, discount, and calculated totals.

9. Pretio Premium and Google Play Billing

Pretio Premium is offered through Google Play as a monthly subscription, a yearly subscription, or a separate lifetime one-time purchase, where available. Google processes the payment. To verify a purchase and manage subscription changes, the app sends the Google Play product ID and purchase token to Pretio's Firebase backend; the backend checks the purchase with the Google Play Developer API. The backend stores a one-way hash of the purchase token and entitlement details such as product, plan, status, and expiry. The original token is used for verification but is not written to the entitlement record. The current source has no automatic deletion TTL for entitlement records, which are retained for verification and purchase lifecycle handling.

Pretio does not directly receive or store your full payment-card details. Google may process purchase information under Google's own terms and privacy policies. For monthly and yearly subscriptions, Google Play determines the active term and expiry. Pretio may keep a previously verified subscription active offline for up to 24 hours, but never beyond the Play expiry; a verified lifetime purchase can remain available offline for up to 7 days. When online, Pretio checks the entitlement again and processes purchase status and revocation notifications from Google Play. Google Play billing events are retained in a separate server event record with a 90-day expiry policy.

A valid Premium entitlement removes Pretio ads, including banner, native, transition interstitial, and rewarded ads; supported saved-data CSV and Shopping List receipt PDF exports do not require a rewarded ad while Premium is active. If a purchase is refunded, revoked, cancelled, reversed, or cannot be verified, Premium access may be removed.

10. Third-party services

Depending on the features you use, third-party services may include Google Firebase and Firebase AI Logic, Cloud Functions for legacy or other supported operations, Open Food Facts, Google Gemini and Google Search, Google AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform, Google Play Billing and the Google Play Developer API, Android link handling, web browsers, marketplace apps, and destination websites opened from Compare Online or Where To Buy. Google Play Billing may also provide the Google Play country used for Pretio's country-level local relevance. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

11. Data security

App-controlled requests to Firebase, Google Play, and advertising services use encrypted network connections. Google states that Google Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit. Your core shopping records are stored locally by Pretio. Earlier versions may have created server-side Product Insight records; purchase-verification records remain on Pretio's Firebase backend. AI requests in current versions are sent through Firebase AI Logic to Google Gemini. No device, storage method, network transmission, or third-party service can be guaranteed to be completely secure. External websites opened from Pretio have their own security and privacy practices.

12. Your choices

You may:

  • use available privacy and consent controls in Pretio;
  • allow or deny Android notification permission; denying it disables the optional inactivity reminder without blocking Pretio's core features;
  • manage advertising settings on your Android device;
  • choose not to use Compare Online;
  • deny device-location permission and continue using the Google Play country for country-level relevance;
  • select or change province, state, region, city, or municipality manually where supported; the country itself follows your Google Play country and is not manually editable in Pretio;
  • use Google, browser, and Android privacy controls where available;
  • choose not to watch a rewarded advertisement;
  • purchase Pretio Premium, where available, to remove Pretio ads and avoid rewarded-ad requirements for supported exports;
  • delete local Pretio records using the app's deletion feature;
  • contact support with a privacy question through the data and privacy information page; and
  • contact us with privacy or purchase questions.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Pretio features, advertising technology, purchase processing, legal requirements, or third-party services change. The updated policy will be published on this page with a revised date.
