Privacy & Data Safety

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 24, 2026 (Pretio 1.5.0) · Last reviewed: November 24, 2026

Local first, cloud sync only if you sign in

Pretio stores your products, saved prices, shopping list, stores, notes, Product Watchlist, and related shopping records on your device. If you sign in with a non-anonymous Google account (optional), your core records are also synced to your Pretio account as described in section 1A. Pretio does not send the contents of those records to AdMob for ad targeting. When you use AI features, requests are routed through Pretio's backend to Google Gemini (primary) and, if unavailable, configured fallback providers (OpenAI and Anthropic) as described in section 4. When you deliberately use Compare Online or Where To Buy, only product information included in the visible Google Shopping search query is sent to Google as described below.

1. Local app data

Pretio does not require an account for local use. Without signing in, your core shopping data—including products, saved prices, shopping list, stores, notes, and your local Product Watchlist (stored in on-device SQLite)—remains on your device unless you choose to use Compare Online, create a backup, export a file, restore a file, or otherwise share data using your device's system features. Compare Online is optional, internet-dependent, and does not run until you select the Online action for a product.

1A. Account, cloud sync, and devices (Pretio 1.5.0)

Pretio lets you sign in with Google to sync your data and use AI features. Signing in is optional for local use and uses Firebase Authentication. Cloud sync, AI features, and purchase verification require a signed-in, non-anonymous Google account; anonymous authentication tokens are rejected by Pretio's backend. We receive your account identifier and the basic profile details Google provides for sign-in (such as email address and name).

When signed in, your products, saved prices, shopping-list items, stores, notes, and related records are stored in Pretio's Firebase (Google Cloud Firestore) project and synced between your devices. Pretio also stores device records (a device identifier, device name or model, last-active time, and whether the device is active), your plan entitlement (Free or Premium), and your AI usage counters. The number of active (syncing) devices is limited: 1 on Free and 3 on Premium. A signed-in device that is not active keeps working locally and can be made active from the Account page.

Items you delete go to Recently Deleted, where they can be restored. You can delete your account from the Account page; the deleted account can be recovered within 30 days. Without signing in, none of this cloud data is created. Cloud data is not used for advertising targeting.

2. Backup, export, restore, and deletion

You can back up Pretio data, export saved product and price-history data as CSV, and export a Shopping List receipt as PDF. A receipt PDF may include the store name you enter, shopping-list items, quantities, prices, subtotal, tax, discount, currency, date, and calculated total. Pretio writes these files only when you choose an export and select a destination through Android's file picker. Once a file leaves Pretio, the storage provider, destination app, or person receiving it may process that file under their own terms and privacy practices.

You can use Delete All Data to remove products, saved prices, your shopping list, stores, Product Watchlist items, and related local records from the device. App preferences such as language, theme, number format, currency, and text size may be preserved as described in the app.

When you are signed in, Delete All Data also deletes your synced cloud data. Backups are manual: Pretio asks whether you want to back up before destructive actions, and nothing is backed up automatically to files. Shared records that are not tied to your account, such as legacy shared Product Insight caches from earlier versions and hashed Google Play purchase-entitlement records, are not removed by Delete All Data. The data and privacy information page explains what can and cannot be identified or removed.

3. Compare Online and Where To Buy

When you select Online for a product, Pretio creates a Google Shopping search query using the current product name, the saved brand when available and not already represented in the product name, the saved package size when available, and the word “price.” That visible query is sent to Google over the internet, and the Google Shopping results page is displayed through an in-app web view.

Where To Buy is a separate, optional Google Shopping search opened from Scan Result. Depending on what is available after a Barcode or Product photo scan, the visible query may include a scanned barcode or an AI-suggested product name, brand, and package size. A photo scan does not require a barcode. Pretio may add the selected country, region, or city as search context. Google receives the visible query and displays its own results; Pretio does not extract or store prices or offers from that page.

Pretio does not include your saved price history, stores, notes, shopping list, Product Watchlist, backups, exports, or locally calculated Best Deal / Best Price in the Compare Online query. Home Best Deal (largest percentage savings compared to your historical average, falling back to your lowest recorded price) and Best Price are calculated solely from your local Pretio records and are not obtained from Google.

Google may process the search query and technical information associated with the request, such as your IP address, device or browser information, cookies or similar technologies, account state when signed in, and interactions with Google Shopping or Google Search, according to Google's policies and your Google settings.

Google-owned shopping or search navigation may remain inside Pretio. When you select an external result, Android may open the destination in a browser, system chooser, website, or compatible marketplace app. That destination may process information under its own privacy policy. Pretio does not control the destination's data practices.

Pretio does not scrape search pages or write visited URLs, search-result content, online prices, or online offers into Pretio's product or price-history database. The web view, browser, Google, Android, or destination service may still retain technical browsing data such as cookies, cache, history, or interaction records under their own controls and policies.

4. Location, local catalog relevance, and AI processing

Pretio detects your country automatically from your device and shows Product Highlights and catalog promos only for that detected country. Country is hidden on purpose and cannot be chosen manually, so you only see promotions for your own country. If no country can be detected, no promos are shown. Within the detected country you can choose a province and city.

Pretio detects the country from your device for each app session and does not store it as a user-editable Country setting. For app functionality, Pretio may cache country-specific Location V2 directory content locally and persist country-scoped canonical identities for selected provinces, states, regions, cities, or municipalities. On a later launch, Pretio detects the country first and then validates saved subdivision identities against that country; cached directory content and saved subdivisions cannot establish Country. This information is not used to create or enhance a user profile or for advertising or marketing targeting. Country is not manually editable in Pretio.

You may select a province, state, region, city, or municipality where supported. If you explicitly tap Use approximate location and grant Android location permission, Android's coarse foreground location may resolve a country, province, and city. Pretio saves the resolved administrative area and its canonical identifiers in local app data and uses it as the active location in later sessions, even if its country differs from the detected country. It remains active until you select another approximate location or clear local app data. Pretio does not store raw device coordinates. Android and its geocoder service may process device location under their own policies. Pretio does not use precise or background location for this feature.

AI-assisted Barcode, Product photo scans, and Multi-Provider AI Fallback (Pretio 1.5.0)

In Pretio 1.5.0, user-triggered AI requests—Barcode Scan Enrichment, Photo Product Identification, and Product Insight—are sent by signed-in (non-anonymous) users through Pretio's Firebase Cloud Functions backend (aiGenerate). To maintain high availability, Pretio's backend uses a Multi-Provider AI Fallback chain:

  • Google Gemini (primary): handles barcode enrichment, product photo vision, and Product Insight (including Google Search Grounding where applicable).
  • OpenAI (fallback): used automatically if the primary provider is unavailable, times out, or returns no usable output.
  • Anthropic (fallback): used automatically if earlier providers in the chain are unavailable or return no usable output.

When you scan an unknown barcode, this user-triggered request may include the barcode, app language, selected country, and relevant locally saved product identity fields (such as name, brand, size, and category when available). Pretio may also check Open Food Facts for structured barcode identity. Pretio does not send your full shopping database, saved price history, stores, notes, Product Watchlist, or shopping list with the AI request.

When you choose Scan > Product and take or select a photo, Pretio compresses it on the device into JPEG (longest side up to 1,280 pixels; outgoing file no larger than 1 MiB) and removes EXIF and GPS metadata before sending the image through Pretio's backend to the active AI provider (Google Gemini primary, or OpenAI / Anthropic on fallback) for product identification. The AI may optionally recognize a barcode from the same photo, but a barcode is not required; a recognizable product may be shown as an AI suggestion without one. Pretio does not save the selected photo to your product records; temporary camera or gallery files are removed on a best-effort basis. Photos are not sent to AdMob for advertising purposes by Pretio.

Product Insight report

Scan Result displays available AI Suggested product information, not a duplicate Product Insight report. The separate Product Insight feature remains available through Product Detail for supported products. When you request it, Pretio sends relevant barcode, country, response language, and applicable product identity context through Pretio's backend to the configured AI provider chain (Google Gemini primary, with OpenAI and Anthropic fallback). Descriptions, source links, product identity, and estimated nutrition can be incomplete or incorrect and must not be treated as verified safety, health, or nutritional advice.

AI quota and result handling (Pretio 1.5.0)

AI product scans, barcode AI, and Product Insight require you to be signed in with a non-anonymous Google account. Free accounts have limited AI usage, while Premium accounts receive higher usage limits. Daily and monthly usage limits may apply and may change over time. Pretio keeps usage counters in your account to enforce these limits; failed AI results are not counted. Results that use Google Search Grounding are shown to you in the app and are never persistently cached or stored by Pretio.

AI storage, external providers, and earlier versions

Pretio does not persist AI-generated responses, Google Search Grounding links, Search Suggestions, or Product Insight reports to shared server caches or saved product records unless you manually save a product or price yourself. This does not mean that external AI providers retain nothing: data handling depends on each provider's service terms and privacy practices. When Google Search Grounding is used, Google may retain prompts, context, and responses for 30 days under its Gemini API terms. When fallback providers process a request, they handle submitted prompts and images under their own policies: OpenAI Privacy Policy, OpenAI API & Business Data, Anthropic Privacy Policy, and Anthropic Privacy Center. Pretio does not claim zero retention by external providers.

Earlier Pretio versions (pre-1.5.0) used legacy client-side Firebase AI Logic flows, the legacy aiConsumeQuota endpoint (now retired and requiring an update to Pretio 1.5.0+), shared Product Insight caches, and request-activity or rate-limit records. Existing legacy shared caches and Google Play purchase-entitlement records may remain on the backend, and their expiry or freshness periods do not guarantee deletion. No individual server-side deletion service has been established for legacy unattributable records; see the data and privacy information page.

Public Product Highlights, catalogs, and app update checks

Pretio does not use a dedicated IP-geolocation service to determine your country and does not send a country-detection request to ipapi.co for this feature. Google Play and other network services may still receive ordinary technical network information, such as your IP address, under their own policies when your device communicates with them.

The supported location directory is bundled in Pretio for offline use. Product Highlights and Catalog Promo content is primarily read from Pretio's static public content hosted on maxqstudio.dev/pretio/ (GitHub Pages), with Pretio's Firebase public-content database as a legacy fallback, and cached locally on your device for 24 hours. Pretio uses the selected country, province, or city context to choose relevant content. GitHub Pages and Firebase receive ordinary technical request information (such as IP address and request headers) when the app fetches remote content. A supported location may have no available catalog or Product Highlights yet.

Pretio uses Firebase Remote Config to check for newer app versions (app_latest_version, app_force_update) and feature availability so the app can display an update prompt or require an update when older versions are retired. Firebase Remote Config receives standard technical metadata (such as app version, platform, and installation instance metadata) under Google's privacy policy.

Pretio's supported language packs are bundled in the app. Pretio does not download language packs from GitHub or the Max-Q Studio website during normal app use.

For Compare Online, Pretio may use the current location context, such as country, province, city, language, or currency, to localize the visible search experience. Pretio does not need to send raw device latitude or longitude to Google Shopping for this purpose. A website opened inside or outside Pretio may have its own separate geolocation request and privacy practices.

5. Advertising

The free version of Pretio may use Google AdMob to display banner, native, app-open, transition interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. A locally stored counter starts with 10 remaining usage units and decreases on eligible transitions: Compare Item uses 1, Compare Category uses 1, Where To Buy uses 2, Compare Online uses 2, and a selected Catalog Promo page uses 1. After an interstitial is dismissed, the counter resets to 10. The counter remains on your device and is not sent to AdMob as product data. An interstitial is shown only when ad eligibility, consent, and ad availability permit. A rewarded advertisement is shown only after you choose to watch it, for example before an eligible saved-data export or Shopping List receipt export.

Google Mobile Ads technology may automatically collect or process information such as:

  • your device's IP address, which may be used to estimate general location;
  • app and advertisement interactions, such as app launches, taps, and video views;
  • diagnostic information about app and advertising-SDK performance; and
  • device or account identifiers, which may include the Android advertising ID or app set ID.

This information may be used by Google for advertising, analytics, security, and fraud-prevention purposes. The exact data handled can depend on the SDK version, your device settings, region, consent choices, and the ad-serving mode.

Learn more through Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising technologies information.

6. Consent and privacy choices

Pretio may use Google's User Messaging Platform to request, store, and update advertising privacy choices where required. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalized, non-personalized, limited, or unavailable.

When required, Pretio will provide a Privacy Options entry in the app so you can review or change available advertising choices. You can also manage or reset your Android advertising ID through your device settings, where supported.

7. Notifications and inactivity reminder

Pretio may ask for Android notification permission. If you grant it, Pretio can schedule a local inactivity reminder on your device approximately 72 hours after the app is opened, reminding you to review saved prices and plan your next shopping list. Opening Pretio again resets that reminder schedule.

The current inactivity reminder is scheduled locally on the device using Android notification functionality; Pretio does not use a remote push-messaging service to deliver this reminder. If you deny notification permission, the reminder is not scheduled and Pretio's core features remain available.

8. Rewarded export

For free users, Pretio may require a completed rewarded advertisement before an eligible export. Current eligible exports include saved product and price-history data as CSV and a Shopping List receipt as PDF. Pretio uses the rewarded-ad completion and dismissal result only to decide whether export authorization is granted.

Pretio does not send the contents of your exported CSV or PDF file to the advertising provider as part of this reward flow. This includes your products, saved prices, stores, notes, shopping-list entries, store name, tax, discount, and calculated totals.

9. Pretio Premium and Google Play Billing

Pretio Premium is offered through Google Play; the price is shown by Google Play before purchase. Premium removes data limits, raises AI limits, and allows 3 active devices. Google processes the payment. To verify a purchase and manage subscription changes, the app sends the Google Play product ID and purchase token from a signed-in (non-anonymous) account to Pretio's Firebase backend; purchase verification requests are rate-limited per account to prevent abuse, and the backend checks the purchase with the Google Play Developer API. The backend stores a one-way hash of the purchase token and entitlement details such as product, plan, status, and expiry. The original token is used for verification but is not written to the entitlement record. The current source has no automatic deletion TTL for entitlement records, which are retained for verification and purchase lifecycle handling.

Pretio does not directly receive or store your full payment-card details. Google may process purchase information under Google's own terms and privacy policies. For monthly and yearly subscriptions, Google Play determines the active term and expiry. Pretio may keep a previously verified subscription active offline for up to 24 hours, but never beyond the Play expiry; a verified lifetime purchase can remain available offline for up to 7 days. When online, Pretio checks the entitlement again and processes purchase status and revocation notifications from Google Play. Google Play billing events are retained in a separate server event record with a 90-day expiry policy.

A valid Premium entitlement removes Pretio ads, including banner, native, transition interstitial, and rewarded ads; supported saved-data CSV and Shopping List receipt PDF exports do not require a rewarded ad while Premium is active. If a purchase is refunded, revoked, cancelled, reversed, or cannot be verified, Premium access may be removed.

10. Third-party services

Depending on the features you use, third-party services may include Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Remote Config, and legacy Firebase AI Logic), Google Gemini and Google Search, OpenAI (AI fallback provider), Anthropic (AI fallback provider), Open Food Facts, GitHub Pages (maxqstudio.dev for static public Product Highlights and catalogs), Google AdMob, Google User Messaging Platform, Google Play Billing and the Google Play Developer API, Android link handling, web browsers, marketplace apps, and destination websites opened from Compare Online or Where To Buy. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

11. Data security

App-controlled requests to Firebase, Google Play, static public content, and advertising services use encrypted HTTPS connections. Google states that Google Mobile Ads SDK data is encrypted in transit. Your core shopping records and Product Watchlist are stored locally by Pretio, and synced cloud records are restricted to your signed-in, non-anonymous account via server-side Cloud Functions. AI requests in Pretio 1.5.0 are routed through Pretio's Firebase Cloud Functions backend to Google Gemini (primary) and, when fallback is triggered, to OpenAI or Anthropic; backend provider routing enforces HTTPS-only endpoints and anti-SSRF safeguards. Earlier versions may have created legacy server-side Product Insight records; purchase-verification records remain on Pretio's Firebase backend. No device, storage method, network transmission, or third-party service can be guaranteed to be completely secure. External websites opened from Pretio have their own security and privacy practices.

12. Your choices

You may:

  • use available privacy and consent controls in Pretio;
  • allow or deny Android notification permission; denying it disables the optional inactivity reminder without blocking Pretio's core features;
  • manage advertising settings on your Android device;
  • choose not to use Compare Online;
  • deny device-location permission and continue using the Google Play country for country-level relevance;
  • select or change province, state, region, city, or municipality manually where supported; the country itself follows your Google Play country and is not manually editable in Pretio;
  • use Google, browser, and Android privacy controls where available;
  • choose not to watch a rewarded advertisement;
  • purchase Pretio Premium, where available, to remove Pretio ads and avoid rewarded-ad requirements for supported exports;
  • delete local Pretio records using the app's deletion feature;
  • contact support with a privacy question through the data and privacy information page; and
  • contact us with privacy or purchase questions.
