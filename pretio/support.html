FAQ

What does Pretio do?

Pretio helps you save product prices, compare options, remember price history, and plan a shopping list.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Pretio does not require login.

Where is my data stored?

Your products, saved prices, and shopping list stay on this device unless you choose to back them up, export them, or restore them yourself.

What is Price Memory?

Price Memory helps you see the best value, product details, and price history at a glance.

What is Compare by Category?

It helps you find the best value in each category more easily.

Can I delete all data?

Yes. You can use Delete All Data to remove products, saved prices, your shopping list, stores, and related records from this device. App preferences such as language, theme, number format, currency, and text size are preserved.

Can I request deletion of server-side data?

You can submit a manual request through the Pretio data deletion request page. Delete All Data in the app only removes local records. The request page explains that shared product caches and purchase-verification records may not be attributable to one installation or may need to be retained.
