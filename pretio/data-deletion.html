Privacy & Data Safety

Pretio Data and Privacy Information

Effective date: the date this information is published · Last reviewed: October 18, 2026

Pretio does not create user accounts. Pretio currently has no operational process for receiving and fulfilling individual requests to delete server-side records. The email below is for privacy questions; it is not a server-side deletion service.

Delete data stored on your device

Use Pretio's Delete All Data feature to remove products, saved prices, stores, shopping lists, and related records stored locally in the app. This does not erase server-side records.

Ask a privacy question

For questions about Pretio's data practices, email pretio.support@maxqstudio.dev. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or Google Play purchase tokens. The email route has not been verified as a server-data deletion process, and Pretio cannot promise deletion of server-side records in response.

What may not be attributable to one person

  • Product Insight reports and product profiles are stored in shared caches keyed by product and market, not by a Pretio account.
  • Some request-activity records contain a barcode and time but no account identity.
  • Request-rate records use a hash of a random installation ID and expire automatically.
  • Purchase-entitlement records use a one-way hash of a Google Play purchase token and support purchase verification, restore, and fraud prevention.

Product Insight caches and diagnostics are shared or request-scoped records without a Pretio account identifier. Request activity records contain a barcode and time, not an installation identity. Rate-limit records use a one-way hash of a random installation ID that the current app does not show to users. Purchase-entitlement records are keyed by a one-way hash of a Google Play purchase token, and the current source has no automatic TTL for them. These structures cannot currently be matched reliably to one person using an email address or order ID. Shared product information is also used by other Pretio users and is not deleted for one installation.

Some request-rate records and barcode-activity records have TTL expiry settings, but Firestore deletion is asynchronous. Cache freshness periods are not deletion periods. The current source does not define a deletion or maximum-retention period for shared Product Insight caches, Gemini diagnostics, or purchase-entitlement records. Pretio therefore does not claim that all server-side data is deleted on request or within 90 days.
