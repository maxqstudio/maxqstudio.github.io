Privacy & Data Safety

Pretio Data Deletion Requests

Draft prepared: October 18, 2026 · Effective date: pending Owner approval

Draft for Owner review. This page is not published. The request route is prepared, but the operator workflow for locating and fulfilling eligible server-data requests has not been verified. Do not submit this page in Play Console until that workflow is approved and tested.

Delete data stored on your device

Use Pretio's Delete All Data feature to remove products, saved prices, stores, shopping lists, and related records stored locally in the app. This does not erase server-side records.

Request a review of server-side data

Pretio does not create a Pretio account. To ask Pretio to review whether server-side records associated with your use can be deleted, send an email to pretio.support@maxqstudio.dev with the subject “Pretio Server Data Deletion Request.” Do not send passwords, payment-card details, or Google Play purchase tokens.

Include only information needed to investigate the request, such as the app version, approximate date of a Product Insight request, barcode involved, and—if the request concerns Premium—your Google Play order ID. These details may help investigate but do not guarantee that a server record can be matched to a particular person or installation.

What may not be attributable to one person

  • Product Insight reports and product profiles are stored in shared caches keyed by product and market, not by a Pretio account.
  • Some request-activity records contain a barcode and time but no account identity.
  • Request-rate records use a hash of a random installation ID and expire automatically.
  • Purchase-entitlement records use a one-way hash of a Google Play purchase token and support purchase verification, restore, and fraud prevention.

Because Pretio has no account linking these records together, Pretio may be unable to identify every record related to a request. Shared product information is not normally removed for one installation because it is used by other Pretio users. Purchase records may be retained or re-created when a valid purchase is verified again. If a record cannot be reliably matched or must be retained for security, fraud prevention, or purchase handling, Pretio will explain the limitation rather than claim it was deleted.

This is a manual request channel, not an automatic deletion function. The final response timeframe and the support team's verification and fulfillment procedure must be confirmed before publication.
