Privacy & Data Safety

Pretio Data and Privacy Information

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Pretio does not create user accounts and does not currently operate a process for receiving and fulfilling requests to delete server-side records. The contact address below is for privacy questions; it is not a server-side deletion service.

Delete data stored on your device

Use Pretio's Delete All Data feature to remove supported products, saved prices, stores, shopping-list entries, and related records stored locally in the app. Photos selected for product identification are not added to product records; Pretio attempts to remove the temporary local copy when the platform permits.

Online requests and provider retention

When you request an AI feature, Pretio may send a barcode, selected country, response language, relevant saved product details, or a photo you explicitly approved to Google Gemini through Firebase AI Logic. Firebase AI Logic itself does not store model inputs or outputs. Google's Gemini API terms describe provider retention; when paid Google Search Grounding is used, Google retains prompts, context, and output for 30 days to provide Grounded Results and Search Suggestions. Pretio does not control provider-side retention.

Records from earlier Pretio versions

Earlier Pretio versions used server-side Product Insight services and may have created shared product or market records, request-activity records, or rate-limit records. Those records are not tied to a Pretio account and may not be attributable to one person. The older deployed service may continue to serve installed versions. Pretio has not verified a general server-side deletion process or a maximum deletion period for every such record.

Google Play purchase verification and subscription lifecycle processing may retain a one-way hash of a purchase token and entitlement status needed to verify, restore, expire, revoke, or prevent misuse of Premium. Pretio does not store full payment-card details. Delete All Data on the device does not delete Google Play purchase-entitlement records.

Ask a privacy question

For questions about Pretio's data practices, email pretio.support@maxqstudio.dev. Do not send passwords, payment-card details, photos, or Google Play purchase tokens. This contact route is not a request-based server deletion mechanism, and Pretio cannot promise deletion of server-side records in response.
