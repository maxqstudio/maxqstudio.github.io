Pretio is a tool for saving product prices, comparing local options, optionally searching for online price information, and planning shopping lists. By using Pretio, you agree to these Terms and Conditions.

1. App purpose

Pretio helps you scan products, save prices, compare local values, optionally search for online information, export saved data, create Shopping List receipt PDFs, and remember useful shopping information. Pretio is not an online marketplace, payment service, financial service, price-verification service, or store operator.

2. Price information and AI features

Saved prices and product information depend on the data you enter or record. Home Best Deal (largest percentage savings compared to your historical average, falling back to your lowest recorded price) and Best Price are calculated solely from your locally saved price history; they are not fetched from Google or another online source. Store prices, promotions, taxes, package sizes, delivery costs, and availability may change. Online search results may also be incomplete, delayed, inaccurate, or shown for a different product variant. Always confirm the seller, product, package size, final price, and purchase terms before buying.

Pretio supports Barcode scans and Product photo scans. In Pretio 1.5.0, AI requests (Barcode Scan Enrichment, Product photo identification, and Product Insight) require a signed-in, non-anonymous Google account and route through Pretio's Firebase Cloud Functions backend (aiGenerate) using a Multi-Provider AI Fallback chain: Google Gemini is the primary provider, and OpenAI and Anthropic are configured fallback providers used automatically if an earlier provider is unavailable, times out, or returns no usable output. Recognizing a barcode is not a requirement to display an AI product suggestion. AI suggestions, descriptions, source links, and estimated nutrition may be incomplete or inaccurate. Verify important information using the product package and reliable sources before making purchases, health, or safety decisions.

Scan Result presents available AI Suggested product information without a duplicate Product Insight report. Product Insight is an optional full product-information report accessed through Product Detail for supported products. It may use Google Gemini (with Google Search Grounding where available), OpenAI, Anthropic, and Open Food Facts; unverified AI-generated information is not official product certification or professional advice. Google Search Grounding results are shown for display only and are never persistently cached by Pretio. These features are not chat services. Each external AI provider handles submitted prompts and images under its own terms and privacy policy (Google Gemini API Terms, OpenAI Business Data, Anthropic Privacy Center). See the Privacy Policy for information sent to providers, photo processing, and retention.

3. Local catalogs, Product Highlights, Watchlist, and location

Pretio includes its supported location directory in the app for offline use. Product Highlights and Catalog Promo content is primarily read from Pretio's static public content hosted on maxqstudio.dev/pretio/ (GitHub Pages), with Pretio's Firebase public-content database as a legacy fallback, and cached on your device for 24 hours. Available links and promotions depend on the supported country, province, or city context and may change without an app update.

You can save Product Highlights to your local Product Watchlist and compare identical products against your saved price history, or prefill Add to Price History and Shopping List entries. Automated product matching and price comparisons are informational only and may not match every variant, pack size, or store condition. Retailers control their own prices, promotions, validity periods, stock, store coverage, websites, and terms. Pretio does not guarantee that a highlight or catalog applies to every store in an area or that a listed promotion is still available. If no catalog is available for a supported location, Pretio may indicate that no catalog is available yet.

Pretio detects your country automatically from your device and shows Product Highlights and catalogs only for that country; country is not user-selectable. If you explicitly choose Use approximate location and grant Android's coarse foreground location permission, Pretio resolves and saves the country and administrative area locally. The saved location becomes active in later app sessions, even if its country differs from Google Play, until you select another location or clear local app data. Pretio does not store raw device coordinates. Android and its geocoder service may process device location under their own policies. Pretio does not use precise or background location for this feature.

Pretio does not use a dedicated IP-geolocation service to determine the country for this feature.

4. Compare Online

Compare Online is an optional, user-initiated feature. No online comparison request is made until you select the Online action for a product.

When you use Compare Online, Pretio creates a Google Shopping search query using the current product name, the saved brand when available and not already represented in the product name, the saved package size when available, and the word “price.” Where To Buy is a separate optional Google Shopping page opened from Scan Result. Depending on what the Barcode or Product photo scan returns, its visible query may use a barcode or an AI-suggested product name, brand, and package size. Product photo recognition does not require a barcode. Pretio may add selected country, region, or city context. Google Shopping results are displayed inside Pretio through an in-app web view. Google-owned shopping or search navigation may remain inside Pretio, while selecting an external result may open a website, browser, Android chooser, or compatible marketplace app outside Pretio.

Pretio does not scrape, extract, verify, rank, or save online offers or online prices from these external search pages. Pretio does not place orders, process transactions, guarantee seller legitimacy, or guarantee that a result matches your exact product. Product Insight does not manage online prices or offers. The Product Name, Brand, Package, and Best Price shown in Pretio remain Pretio data. Best Price is calculated only from your locally saved price records.

External results, sellers, websites, and apps are operated by third parties. Their content, prices, availability, delivery terms, account requirements, tracking, security, and transactions are governed by their own terms and policies. You are responsible for verifying the seller, product variant, package size, final price, and purchase terms before buying.

5. Local data and backups

Your product records, saved prices, shopping list, notes, Product Watchlist, and related shopping data are stored locally on your device unless you choose optional cloud sync, an online AI feature, Compare Online, Where To Buy, a backup, export, restore, or sharing action. Barcode Scan Enrichment and Product Insight may send a barcode, selected country, response language, and relevant saved product identity through Pretio's backend to Google Gemini (primary) or, on fallback, OpenAI or Anthropic. Product photo identification sends a compressed user-selected image (with EXIF/GPS metadata removed) through Pretio's backend to the active AI provider. Compare Online and Where To Buy send the product information shown in the search query to Google. These features do not upload your full product database, saved price history, stores, notes, Product Watchlist, shopping list, backups, or exports. See the Privacy Policy for service and retention details.

Pretio can export saved product and price-history data as CSV and can create a Shopping List receipt as PDF. Export files are saved only after you choose a destination through Android's file picker. Once a backup or export is outside Pretio, the selected storage provider, destination app, or recipient may apply their own terms and privacy practices.

If you uninstall Pretio or clear its data without a backup, your records may be lost. Once a backup or export file is saved outside Pretio, you are responsible for where it is stored, who can access it, and whether it remains available.

Delete All Data removes local app records and, when you are signed in, your synced cloud data. Backups are manual. It does not remove legacy shared Product Insight caches from earlier app versions or Google Play purchase-verification records. The data and privacy information page describes these limits and provides a support contact for privacy questions.

6. Free version and advertising

The free version of Pretio may display banner ads, native ads, transition interstitials, and rewarded ads. A locally stored counter starts with 10 remaining usage units and decreases on eligible transitions: Compare Item uses 1, Compare Category uses 1, Where To Buy uses 2, Compare Online uses 2, and a selected Catalog Promo page uses 1. After an interstitial is dismissed, the counter resets to 10. Pretio uses this counter only to time interstitials; it remains on your device. Interstitial availability depends on Google AdMob, your device, internet connection, consent choices, region, and available ad inventory.

In the free version, an eligible export may require you to voluntarily watch a rewarded advertisement before the export is authorized. Current eligible exports include saved product and price-history data as CSV and a Shopping List receipt as PDF. Pretio explains this before showing the ad, and export authorization is granted only after the rewarded-ad flow confirms the required completion.

If a rewarded advertisement cannot load, is closed early, or does not return a completion confirmation, the free export may not be available at that time. Pretio does not guarantee that an advertisement will always be available.

6A. Free plan limits and AI quota

The free plan includes up to 50 products, 50 prices per product, 50 shopping-list items, limited daily and monthly AI scan usage, and 1 active device. AI features require a signed-in, non-anonymous Google account. If you reach or exceed a data limit (for example when Premium ends), Pretio never deletes your data automatically; adding new items is blocked until you delete items or upgrade. AI results that fail are not counted toward quota. Limits may be adjusted, and AI features depend on the availability of Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, and Firebase services.

It is not an official retailer receipt, fiscal receipt, tax invoice, proof of payment, warranty document, or confirmation that a store charged or accepted the listed amounts. You are responsible for checking the entered values, calculations, and final purchase information against the actual retailer transaction.
