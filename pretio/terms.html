Pretio is a tool for saving product prices, comparing local options, optionally searching for online price information, and planning shopping lists. By using Pretio, you agree to these Terms and Conditions.

1. App purpose

Pretio helps you scan products, save prices, compare local values, optionally search for online information, export saved data, create Shopping List receipt PDFs, and remember useful shopping information. Pretio is not an online marketplace, payment service, financial service, price-verification service, or store operator.

2. Price information

Saved prices and product information depend on the data you enter or record. The Best Price shown by Pretio is calculated from your locally saved price history; it is not fetched from Google or another online source. Store prices, promotions, taxes, package sizes, delivery costs, and availability may change. Online search results may also be incomplete, delayed, inaccurate, or shown for a different product variant. Always confirm the seller, product, package size, final price, and purchase terms before buying.

Product Insight is an optional product-information report. It may use public product sources, Pretio's shared product cache, and Google Gemini. From Scan Result, an image search may run automatically when product identity is verified but an image is missing; a result is shown only with an available source. Gemini text may appear even when external Search evidence is unavailable; in that case, the text is not externally verified and is labeled as AI-generated in the app. Product Insight is not a chat service, official product certification, health or nutrition advice, or a guarantee of product identity. Check source links and the package label. Details about information sent to these services are in the Privacy Policy.

3. Local catalogs and location

Pretio includes its supported location directory in the app for offline use. Catalog Promo and Product Highlight content may be read from Pretio's Firebase public-content database and cached on your device for 24 hours. Available links and promotions depend on the supported country, province, or city context and may change without an app update.

Catalog availability is informational only. Retailers control their own prices, promotions, validity periods, stock, store coverage, websites, and terms. Pretio does not guarantee that a catalog applies to every store in an area or that a listed promotion is still available. If no catalog is available for a supported location, Pretio may indicate that no catalog is available yet.

Pretio uses the country selected for your Google Play account as the initial country context. This is not necessarily your current physical location. If you explicitly choose Use approximate location and grant Android's coarse foreground location permission, Pretio resolves and saves the country and administrative area locally. The saved location becomes active in later app sessions, even if its country differs from Google Play, until you select another location or clear local app data. Pretio does not store raw device coordinates. Android and its geocoder service may process device location under their own policies. Pretio does not use precise or background location for this feature.

Pretio does not use a dedicated IP-geolocation service to determine the country for this feature.

4. Compare Online

Compare Online is an optional, user-initiated feature. No online comparison request is made until you select the Online action for a product.

When you use Compare Online, Pretio creates a Google Shopping search query using the current product name, the saved brand when available and not already represented in the product name, the saved package size when available, and the word “price.” Where To Buy is a separate optional Google Shopping page opened from Scan Result. Before a verified product identity is available, its product identifier in the query is the scanned barcode. When verified identity is available, the query may also include the brand, product name, package size, and barcode. Pretio may add selected country, region, or city context. Google Shopping results are displayed inside Pretio through an in-app web view. Google-owned shopping or search navigation may remain inside Pretio, while selecting an external result may open a website, browser, Android chooser, or compatible marketplace app outside Pretio.

Pretio does not scrape, extract, verify, rank, or save online offers or online prices from these external search pages. Pretio does not place orders, process transactions, guarantee seller legitimacy, or guarantee that a result matches your exact product. Product Insight does not manage online prices or offers. The Product Name, Brand, Package, and Best Price shown in Pretio remain Pretio data. Best Price is calculated only from your locally saved price records.

External results, sellers, websites, and apps are operated by third parties. Their content, prices, availability, delivery terms, account requirements, tracking, security, and transactions are governed by their own terms and policies. You are responsible for verifying the seller, product variant, package size, final price, and purchase terms before buying.

5. Local data and backups

Your product records, saved prices, shopping list, notes, and related shopping data are stored locally on your device unless you deliberately request Product Insight, use Compare Online or Where To Buy, back them up, export them, restore them, or otherwise share them yourself. Product Insight may send the barcode, language, and selected market context to Pretio's backend; when requested for a saved product, its name, brand, package size, and category may be included as context. Compare Online and Where To Buy send the product information shown in the search query to Google. These features do not upload your full product database, saved price history, stores, notes, shopping list, backups, or exports. See the Privacy Policy for service and retention details.

Pretio can export saved product and price-history data as CSV and can create a Shopping List receipt as PDF. Export files are saved only after you choose a destination through Android's file picker. Once a backup or export is outside Pretio, the selected storage provider, destination app, or recipient may apply their own terms and privacy practices.

If you uninstall Pretio or clear its data without a backup, your records may be lost. Once a backup or export file is saved outside Pretio, you are responsible for where it is stored, who can access it, and whether it remains available.

Delete All Data removes local app records; it does not remove shared Product Insight caches or Google Play purchase-verification records. Pretio currently has no operational process for deleting server-side records on an individual request. The data and privacy information page describes these limits and provides a support contact for privacy questions.

6. Free version and advertising

The free version of Pretio may display banner ads, native ads, transition interstitials, and rewarded ads. A locally stored counter starts with 10 remaining usage units and decreases on eligible transitions: Compare Item uses 1, Compare Category uses 1, Where To Buy uses 2, Compare Online uses 2, and a selected Catalog Promo page uses 1. After an interstitial is dismissed, the counter resets to 10. Pretio uses this counter only to time interstitials; it remains on your device. Interstitial availability depends on Google AdMob, your device, internet connection, consent choices, region, and available ad inventory.

In the free version, an eligible export may require you to voluntarily watch a rewarded advertisement before the export is authorized. Current eligible exports include saved product and price-history data as CSV and a Shopping List receipt as PDF. Pretio explains this before showing the ad, and export authorization is granted only after the rewarded-ad flow confirms the required completion.

If a rewarded advertisement cannot load, is closed early, or does not return a completion confirmation, the free export may not be available at that time. Pretio does not guarantee that an advertisement will always be available.

8. Shopping List receipt PDF

Pretio can generate a Shopping List receipt PDF from information in your local shopping list, including the store name you enter, item names, quantities, prices, subtotal, optional tax or discount, currency, date, and calculated total.

The Shopping List receipt PDF is a planning and record-keeping document generated by Pretio. It is not an official retailer receipt, fiscal receipt, tax invoice, proof of payment, warranty document, or confirmation that a store charged or accepted the listed amounts. You are responsible for checking the entered values, calculations, and final purchase information against the actual retailer transaction.
