Questions

It does not scream when the screen is off.

Make sure background protection is on and its notification is visible. Some phones stop background apps to save battery: in Android settings, open Apps, Argh!, Battery, and allow it to run in the background (Unrestricted).

It screams too easily, or not easily enough.

Each action (drop, flip, pick-up) has its own sensitivity slider from 0 to 100. Open Customize for that action to change it, or switch off the triggers you don't need.

Does it drain my battery?

Argh! only reacts when the phone moves and keeps the work very small. Tapping Stop in the notification, or swiping Argh! away from recent apps, stops it completely.

How do I turn on Missed call or Unread alerts?

Open the option in the app. You will see a short explanation first, then Android's notification access screen. Argh! only checks which app a notification comes from and its category. It never reads titles or message text. Turn it off any time in Android settings.

Can each action have a different scream?

Yes. Open Customize for drop, flip or pick-up and choose its own voice and sensitivity. Voices include kid, diva, deep, goofy, grandpa and cartoon.

How do I add my own scream (My Sound)?

Add a My Sound, then either tap Record or pick an audio file with Android's file picker, and give it a name. Free users get 3 My Sound slots, Premium gets unlimited slots. The microphone is used only while you tap Record, and sounds stay on your phone.

It stopped working after I restarted my phone.

Argh! does not start by itself after a restart, to save battery and respect your control. Open the app and turn protection on again.

What are phone weight and case for?

They are optional. Enter your phone's approximate weight and case type so Argh! can fit its motion detection to your phone. They stay on your phone and are never sent anywhere. Leave them empty to use an average phone.

How do I stop it?

Tap Stop in the ongoing notification, switch protection off in the app, or swipe Argh! away from recent apps. It stops fully.

How do I restore Premium on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Google account, open Premium in the app and tap Restore purchase.

Can I change the language?

Argh! follows your phone's language. On Android 13 and later you can choose a language just for Argh! in Settings, Apps, Argh!, Language.
