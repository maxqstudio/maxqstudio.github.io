Questions

It does not scream when the screen is off.

Make sure background protection is on and its notification is visible. Some phones stop background apps to save battery: in Android settings, open Apps, Argh!, Battery, and allow it to run in the background (Unrestricted).

It screams too easily, or not easily enough.

Change the sensitivity in the app, or switch off the triggers you don't need.

Does it drain my battery?

Argh! only reacts when the phone moves and keeps the work very small. Turning protection off from the notification stops it completely.

How do I stop it?

Tap Stop in the ongoing notification, or switch protection off in the app.

How do I restore Premium on a new phone?

Sign in with the same Google account, open Premium in the app and tap Restore purchase.

Can I change the language?

Argh! follows your phone's language. On Android 13 and later you can choose a language just for Argh! in Settings, Apps, Argh!, Language.
