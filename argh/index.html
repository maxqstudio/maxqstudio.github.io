About the app

Argh!Scream Phone

Drop it, flip it, pick it up: your phone screams. A tiny Android app with a loud mouth, built to sip battery and stay out of your way.

Get it on Google Play Rate us
AAARGH!

Three ways to scream

Turn on the triggers you want. Argh! listens to the motion sensors only, and only reacts when the phone actually moves.

Drop!

When it falls

A free fall is caught in a split second, so the scream starts before the phone lands.

Flip!

When it is turned over

Flip the phone face down or upside down and it lets you know about it.

Grab!

When it is picked up

Someone lifts your phone off the table? Argh!

Light and battery friendly

  • Works in the background with a small ongoing notification, as Android requires.
  • Motion is checked on the phone itself. Nothing about your movements is saved or sent anywhere.
  • No account, no sign-in, no microphone, camera or location access.

Premium, one-time

The free version shows a small banner ad and an app-open ad. A single Premium purchase on Google Play removes the ads for good and unlocks the Premium extras shown in the app. No subscription.

Argh! is made for fun. It is not a security or anti-theft system, and it cannot stop a phone from breaking. Please don't throw your phone to test it.
