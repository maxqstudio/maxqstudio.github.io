About the app

Argh!Scream Phone

Drop it, flip it, pick it up: your phone screams. A tiny Android app with a loud mouth, built to sip battery and stay out of your way.

Get it on Google Play Rate us
AAARGH!

Three ways to scream

Turn on the triggers you want. Argh! uses the motion sensors (and, optionally, the proximity sensor for pocket detection) and only reacts when the phone actually moves.

Drop!

When it falls

A free fall is caught in a split second, so the scream starts before the phone lands.

Flip!

When it is turned over

Flip the phone face down or upside down and it lets you know about it.

Grab!

When it is picked up

Someone lifts your phone off the table? Argh!

Make it yours

Every trigger can scream its own way. Pick a voice, set how touchy it is, or use a sound of your own.

Custom!

Per-action customize

Drop, flip and pick-up each get their own scream and their own sensitivity, so a gentle lift and a hard fall don't have to sound the same.

Tune!

Sensitivity 0 to 100

A simple slider from "barely moved" to "only a real fall". Set it once per action and forget it.

Voices!

Six extra voices

Kid, diva, deep, goofy, grandpa and cartoon, on top of the classic scream.

Your own sounds

  • Free: 2 custom sound slots. Import any audio file you have the right to use.
  • Premium: unlimited custom sound slots.
  • Premium: record your own scream with the microphone (only while you tap Record).

Stays on your phone

Imported and recorded sounds are kept in Argh!'s private storage. They are never uploaded.

Light and battery friendly

  • Works in the background with a small ongoing notification, as Android requires.
  • Motion is checked on the phone itself. Nothing about your movements is saved or sent anywhere.
  • No account and no sign-in. No camera, location, contacts, call log or phone-state access.
  • Optional extras (recording your own scream, missed call and unread alerts) ask for permission first and are off until you turn them on.

Premium, one-time

The free version shows a small banner ad and an app-open ad, and an optional rewarded video can unlock one advanced action for 24 hours. A single Premium purchase on Google Play removes the ads for good, gives you unlimited custom sound slots and lets you record your own scream. No subscription.

Argh! is made for fun. It is not a security or anti-theft system, and it cannot stop a phone from breaking. Please don't throw your phone to test it.
