2. What the app does

Argh! plays a scream sound when your phone's sensors detect a fall, a flip or a pick-up (and optionally a missed call or unread notification alert), depending on the triggers you turn on. You can fill a My Sound slot by recording your own scream or picking an audio file, and give it a name. Optionally you can enter your phone's approximate weight and case type so detection fits your phone better; these are estimates and the app works without them. Protection runs only while you have turned it on: it stops when you tap Stop or swipe the app away, and it does not start again by itself after a restart. Detection depends on your device's sensors and on Android's background and battery rules, so it may sometimes react late, react to ordinary movement, or not react at all.

4. Use it responsibly

  • Do not drop or throw your phone to try the app. Damage to your device is your responsibility.
  • Screams can be loud. Keep the volume at a level that is safe for your hearing and for people around you.
  • Do not use the app to frighten, harass or distract people, especially drivers, the elderly, young children, animals, or anyone in a situation where a sudden sound could be dangerous.
  • Follow local rules about noise and respect quiet places.
  • Only record or pick sounds you have the right to use, and do not record other people without their consent.
