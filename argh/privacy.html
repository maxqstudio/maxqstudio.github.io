Privacy Policy

Argh!: Scream Phone for Android (com.maxq.argh) · Effective November 13, 2026 (app version 1.0.6)

In short

Argh! has no account and no server of its own. Sensor readings are processed on your phone, in the moment, and are never stored or sent. Your settings and any recording stay on your device. Argh! never uploads audio. The free version shows Google AdMob ads, which collect data as described below.

1. Who we are

Argh!: Scream Phone ("Argh!", "the app") is published by Max-Q Studio. Contact: support@maxqstudio.dev.

2. Sensors

To notice when your phone falls, is turned over or is picked up, Argh! reads the accelerometer while protection is switched on. If you enable pocket detection, it also reads the proximity sensor. The readings are evaluated on the device and discarded right away. Argh! does not record, store, upload or share sensor data, and does not use it to identify you or track your activity.

3. Data stored on your device

Argh! saves your settings in the app's private storage: which triggers are on, the selected scream sound and volume options, sensitivity, whether background protection is on, your notification-alert choices, a cached Premium status, rewarded-unlock timing, your per-action voice and sensitivity choices, and your My Sound names, recordings and picked sounds if you add them. If you choose to enter them, it also saves your phone's approximate weight (in grams) and case type (none, slim or thick). These two optional values are only estimates you type or tap, are used only on the phone to adjust how the motion sensors are interpreted (for example how hard a knock must be), and are never sent, shared or used for advertising. Leave them empty and Argh! uses an average phone. This data is not sent to Max-Q Studio. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

4. Permissions

  • Foreground service and notifications: keep protection running in the background, with an ongoing notification so you always know it is on. Protection stops completely when you tap Stop or swipe Argh! away from recent apps.
  • No auto-start: Argh! does not start by itself when the phone restarts. Protection stays off until you open the app and turn it on again.
  • Microphone (only when you tap Record): used solely to record your own My Sound scream. Nothing is recorded at any other time. Recordings are limited to a few seconds, stay in Argh!'s private storage on your device and are never uploaded or shared.
  • Notification access (optional): only if you turn on the Missed call or Unread alert actions, and only after a clear in-app notice asking for your consent. Argh! reads just the notification's app package, category, channel and flags to decide whether to react. It never reads the title or text of any notification, and nothing is stored or sent. You can turn it off in Android settings at any time.
  • Internet and network state, and advertising ID: used by Google AdMob, Google's consent tools and Google Play Billing.

Argh! does not request call log or phone-state permissions, and does not access your location, camera, contacts, photos, shared storage or accounts. If you pick an audio file for a My Sound, you pick the file with Android's file picker and Argh! copies it into its private storage, so no storage permission is needed. My Sound files and recordings are stored only on your device: 3 slots on the free version, unlimited with Premium. You can rename, replace or delete them in the app at any time.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a banner ad and may show an app-open ad when you open or return to the app. You can also choose to watch a rewarded video to unlock one advanced action for 24 hours. Google Mobile Ads may collect or process your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), the advertising ID (Argh! declares the AD_ID permission) or app set ID, ad interactions and diagnostic information, for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in ads.

6. Consent and privacy choices

Argh! uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for advertising consent where the law requires it, for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. When required, a Privacy choices button is available in the app so you can change your decision. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

7. Premium purchase (Google Play)

Premium is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. The product is called argh_premium. Argh! receives the purchase status from Google Play on your device to unlock Premium and remove ads, and caches that status locally. You can use Restore purchase in the app on a new device. Argh! never receives your payment details.

8. Ratings and links

The Rate Us option in the app opens this website's Rate Us page in your browser, which links to Google Play. Your rating and review go to Google Play; Argh! does not see them. Links from the app to this website open in your browser without any sign-in.

9. Children

Argh! is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. Data sharing and security

Max-Q Studio does not sell or share personal data and stores no personal data on its own servers. Network traffic from Google's SDKs uses encrypted connections.

11. Changes

If Argh!'s data practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy: support@maxqstudio.dev.
