Privacy Policy

Argh!: Scream Phone for Android (com.maxq.argh) · Effective November 12, 2026

In short

Argh! has no account and no server of its own. Motion sensor readings are processed on your phone, in the moment, and are never stored or sent. Your settings stay on your device. The free version shows Google AdMob ads, which collect data as described below.

1. Who we are

Argh!: Scream Phone ("Argh!", "the app") is published by Max-Q Studio. Contact: support@maxqstudio.dev.

2. Motion sensors

To notice when your phone falls, is turned over or is picked up, Argh! reads the phone's motion sensors (such as the accelerometer) while protection is switched on. The readings are evaluated on the device and discarded right away. Argh! does not record, store, upload or share sensor data, and does not use it to identify you or track your activity.

3. Data stored on your device

Argh! saves your settings in the app's private storage: which triggers are on, the selected scream sound and volume options, sensitivity, whether background protection is on, a cached Premium status, and simple counters used to decide when to ask for a review. This data is not sent to Max-Q Studio. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

4. Permissions

  • Foreground service and notifications: keep protection running in the background, with an ongoing notification so you always know it is on and can stop it.
  • Run at startup (if you enable it): turns protection back on after the phone restarts.
  • Internet and network state: used only by Google AdMob, Google's consent tools and Google Play Billing.

Argh! does not request microphone, camera, location, contacts, photos, storage or account access.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a banner ad and may show an app-open ad when you open or return to the app. Google Mobile Ads may collect or process your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), the advertising ID or app set ID, ad interactions and diagnostic information, for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in ads.

6. Consent and privacy choices

Argh! uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for advertising consent where the law requires it, for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland. When required, a Privacy choices option is available in the app so you can change your decision. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

7. Premium purchase (Google Play)

Premium is a one-time purchase processed by Google Play. Argh! receives the purchase status from Google Play on your device to unlock Premium and remove ads, and caches that status locally. Argh! never receives your payment details.

8. Ratings and links

The Rate Us option opens Google Play's review screen or the app's Google Play page. Your rating and review go to Google Play; Argh! does not see them. Links to this website open in your browser without any sign-in.

9. Children

Argh! is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

10. Data sharing and security

Max-Q Studio does not sell or share personal data and stores no personal data on its own servers. Network traffic from Google's SDKs uses encrypted connections.

11. Changes

If Argh!'s data practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy: support@maxqstudio.dev.
