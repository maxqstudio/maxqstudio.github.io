Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026 · Android & iOS app “Max Cam Scanner” (com.maxq.scanner)

In short: document photos and OCR are processed on your phone by default. If you choose AI mode, selected page images are sent through our server to configured AI provider(s) to create editable Word or Excel output. Sharing or saving to a workspace also sends data off your device. Ads are served by Google AdMob.

1. Who we are

Max Cam Scanner is made by Max-Q Studio (“we”). Privacy questions can be sent to scanner.support@maxqstudio.dev.

2. Data processed on your device

  • Camera and photos. The camera is used only when you tap Scan. Gallery photos and PDF files are read only when you pick them.
  • Scans, OCR results, annotations, watermarks, and signatures. These are stored in the app's storage on your phone. Text recognition (OCR) uses Google ML Kit running on the device, so OCR itself does not send images to a server. AI mode is different: when you choose it, selected page images are sent for AI processing as described in section 5.
  • Signatures you draw are stored as images on your phone. They are not certified electronic signatures.
  • Password attempt lockout state. For email-and-password accounts, the App stores a local failed-attempt counter and temporary block expiry timestamp on your phone to enforce progressive blocks (5 wrong attempts → 5 minutes, then 15 minutes, 60 minutes, and up to 24 hours max; another wrong attempt after 24 hours blocks for 24 hours again) during sign-in and password change. This state is cleared automatically after a successful password entry or password reset.

Deleting a document or the app removes that data from the device.

3. Data we process when you sign in

You can use the app without an account. Shared workspaces require a Max Cam Scanner account. The App is standalone: its accounts, subscriptions, and workspace data are not connected or synced with maxq.tools. If you sign in, we process:

  • Account: your name, email address, profile photo, and user ID from Google, through Firebase Authentication.
  • Workspaces: workspace name, member list with emails and roles (Owner, Editor, Viewer), file metadata such as title, type, size, time, and uploader, and the identity of the member currently editing a file, stored in Google Cloud Firestore or Cloudflare D1.
  • Workspace files: PDF, DOC, and Excel files you save to a workspace, stored in Cloud Storage for Firebase or Cloudflare R2 and accessible only to that workspace's members.
  • Invites: the email of the person you invite, the role offered, and the invite status (including whether the invited person blocked invites from you). We send one invitation email to that address at your request.
  • Collaboration: comments on workspace files, who was tagged (@mentions), signature requests and their status, and every saved version of a file (who saved it, when, an optional note, and a summary of what changed). Every saved version of a file is kept until a member with permission deletes it, deletes the file, or the workspace is deleted. A rollback permanently removes the newer versions after confirmation.
  • Synced settings: signatures you draw while signed in and the last watermark you used, so they are available on your other phones. Signatures are removed from a phone when you sign out of it.
  • Notifications: in-app notifications about mentions and signature requests, and a Firebase Cloud Messaging installation ID per phone so we can send push notifications. You can turn push notifications off in your phone settings.
  • Plan and storage: your plan (Free, Pro, Pro Team, Max, or Max Team), how much storage you and each workspace use, and any storage limit an Owner sets for a workspace.
  • AI usage: your user ID, plan, page counts by day and month, and the AI provider/model used may appear in quota records and operational logs. We use this information to enforce usage limits, show your usage, operate, and troubleshoot the feature.

This data is processed by Google Firebase (Google LLC), including Firebase Functions in Jakarta, Indonesia (asia-southeast2); Cloudflare, Inc. (Workers, D1, and R2) in its Asia-Pacific region; and, when you choose AI mode, the AI provider(s) configured for the feature. AI providers may include OpenAI, Anthropic, or another configured provider. Data may be handled on servers outside your country. Sign-in always goes through Firebase Authentication.

4. Ads

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner, an ad when you open the app, and a rewarded ad before export. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information, and ad interactions. Where required, we ask for your consent first through Google's consent form. You can reset or opt out of ad personalisation in your device settings. Pro users see no ads. See how Google uses data.

5. AI features

AI mode is optional and available on eligible paid plans. When you select it to convert pages into editable Word or Excel output, the App sends the selected page images to a Firebase Function in Jakarta. Images are resized and compressed before sending and may contain personal or sensitive information. Up to 10 page images may be sent in one request. The Function forwards the images to one or more AI providers enabled in our server configuration; this may include OpenAI, Anthropic, or another configured provider. If a provider fails, the service may send the same images to the next enabled provider. The AI result is returned to the App as a local document draft; it is not uploaded to a workspace unless you explicitly save it there.

We record account-linked page usage to enforce daily and monthly limits and show your allowance. Each AI provider handles submitted images under its own terms and privacy practices, which may include processing or retention outside your country. See OpenAI's business data privacy information and the Anthropic Privacy Center. We cannot promise the same retention or training settings for every provider or a separately configured provider. Avoid sending highly sensitive information unless you are comfortable with that provider's terms.

6. How we use data

  • To run the features you choose: sign-in, workspaces, sharing, invites, comments, signature requests, version history, and notifications.
  • To enforce roles and storage limits.
  • To show ads in the free version.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse.

We do not sell your personal data and do not use the contents of your documents for advertising.

7. Sharing

Files in a workspace can be seen by all of its members. Workspace members can also see which member currently holds the editing session for a file. If you choose AI mode, the selected page images are also shared with our server and the AI provider(s) configured for the feature. Other data is shared with the service providers listed above or when required by law.

8. Retention & deletion

  • Workspace files and their versions are kept until an Owner or Co-owner deletes the file (which removes all its versions and comments), or the Owner deletes the workspace.
  • Invites expire after 7 days.
  • To delete your account and its data, follow the steps on Delete account & data.

9. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Workspace access is limited by security rules that check membership and role on every request. Email-and-password sign-in and password changes are additionally protected by progressive temporary blocks after repeated wrong password attempts (5 wrong attempts → 5 minutes, then 15 minutes, 60 minutes, and up to 24 hours max). No system is 100% secure, so think twice before storing highly sensitive documents.

10. Children

The app is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

11. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, or delete your personal data under applicable law, including Indonesia's Personal Data Protection Law (Law No. 27 of 2022). Contact us at the email above.

12. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date on this page is updated. For important changes, we also let you know in the app. This update explains local password attempt lockout state, AI mode data processing, and the separation of Max Cam Scanner accounts, plans, and workspaces from maxq.tools.
