Why Max Cam Scanner

From paper to an editable file in a few taps.

Simple on a phone screen, without leaving features out.

01

Clean scans, automatically

Edges are detected, cropped, and straightened. Scan many pages at once, then reorder, rotate, or delete them.

02

Import photos & PDFs

Already took the photo? Pick it from your gallery. Got a PDF? Every page can be read, marked up, and exported again.

03

PDF, DOC, or Excel

Pick the result: a PDF ready to send, an editable Word document, or an Excel table from receipts and lists.

04

Sign, annotate, watermark

Pen, highlighter, and text in several colours, plus watermarks. Draw your signature once, then place and resize it on any page.

05

Light editors

Tidy paragraphs and headings in DOC, or edit cells, rows, and columns in Excel, comfortably with one thumb.

06

Shared workspaces

Keep files for your team. Invite by email as Co-owner, Editor, Commenter, or Viewer, with clear storage limits.

07

Ask for a signature

Comment on a file and tag your manager with @. Turn on “Request signature” and they get a push, an email, and a to-do in the app.

08

Version history

Every save is a version, like a commit: who, when, a note, and what changed. Owners can roll back without losing history.

09

Notifications

Mentions, signature requests, and “signed” updates arrive as push notifications and in your inbox. Signatures and watermarks follow your account.
