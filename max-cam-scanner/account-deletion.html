Delete your account & data

You can delete your Max Cam Scanner account at any time. If you only used the app as a guest, there is no account: uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes everything.

Option 1: in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Max Cam Scanner and go to Account.
  2. Under Danger zone, tap Delete account.
  3. Read what will be deleted, tick both boxes (including the subscription note), type DELETE and wait for the 10-second countdown.
  4. Tap Delete permanently. Your account and its data are removed right away.

Option 2: by email

  1. Email scanner.support@maxqstudio.dev from the Google account address you use.
  2. Use the subject "Max Cam Scanner - Delete account".
  3. We may ask you to confirm from the same address, then complete it within 30 days.
Send request

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in (the Firebase account linked to your Google account), name, email, and profile photo.
  • Workspaces you own, with all their files and members' access.
  • Your membership in other people's workspaces. Files you uploaded there stay with that workspace until an Owner or Editor deletes them.
  • Invites you sent or received.

What is not affected

  • Scans and files on your phone. Delete them in the app or uninstall it to remove them.
  • Ad data managed by Google under Google's policies. You can reset your advertising ID in your phone settings.
  • A Pro subscription (if any) is not cancelled automatically. Cancel it in Google Play, under Payments & subscriptions.

Deletion is permanent and workspace files cannot be restored.
