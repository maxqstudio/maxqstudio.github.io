1. The service

Eligible paid plans also include an optional AI mode for converting selected page images into editable Word documents or Excel tables.

3. Workspaces, roles, and storage

  • Owner: everything, including deleting the workspace. Co-owner: the same as the owner (members, roles, invites, deleting files, rollback) except deleting the workspace or changing the owner. Editor: add files and save new versions. Commenter: open files, comment, and request signatures. Viewer: open and download only.
  • Every save creates a version. Deleting a file removes all of its versions and comments and cannot be undone. A rollback makes an older version the current one. After you confirm, all newer versions are permanently deleted from the history and cannot be restored. There is no limit on the number of versions; they count toward storage.
  • Free users have one workspace of their own with 50 MB of storage. Paid plans have one storage pool per Owner: 25 GB for Pro and Pro Team, 100 GB for Max and Max Team, shared by all of the Owner's workspaces, with no limit on the number of workspaces. The Owner may set a storage limit for each workspace; the limits together cannot exceed the pool, and a save that would pass a workspace's limit is refused (existing versions are never deleted). Files uploaded by members count against the Owner's storage.
  • Members per workspace depend on the Owner's plan. Members plus pending invites, not counting the Owner, may total: Free up to 2 people (1 workspace, 50 MB), Pro up to 10, Max up to 25, and unlimited for Pro Team and Max Team. Team plans also include 4 plan seats (Pro or Max for you plus 4 more people on your plan), which is separate from workspace membership.
  • Invites are sent by email and can only be accepted, within 7 days, by the account with the same email (Google, or email and password). The invited person can block invites from an Owner; blocking hides the invite on their side and does not notify the Owner, who keeps seeing it as Pending.
  • Owners are responsible for who they invite. All members can see the files in a workspace.

Editing a workspace file. While a file is open in its editor, one member at a time holds the editing session; other workspace members can see who is editing. Your changes are first kept in a local working cache. The App may autosave to this cache, but this does not update the workspace. Only an explicit Save to Workspace uploads the file and creates a new remote version. The cache remains after that save and is hidden from Documents. Save to Device creates a separate local document.

When you enter or re-enter the editor, the App checks your signed-in account and this device's saved session ID, then checks the latest workspace version. The same device can recover its own session; another device, even with the same account, cannot take over that active session. If the workspace version changed, the old unsaved cache is cancelled and replaced with the latest version. Your cache is resumed only when its base version is still current. The App does not merge concurrent edits or promise simultaneous editing.

Each workspace save also checks that the version you started from is still current. If it has changed, the save is refused to prevent overwriting the newer version.

The server session expires 60 seconds after the last successful renewal. While the editor is active, the App sends a lightweight heartbeat every 30 seconds; it does not download the document, model, or blobs. Back, a successful workspace save, and normal app backgrounding or closing stop renewals and attempt to release the session right away. A force-stop, crash, power loss, or connectivity failure may prevent immediate release or detection; if renewals stop, the session expires automatically no later than 60 seconds after the last successful renewal.
