Help for Max Fake Location on Android

Setup in four steps

  1. Enable Developer options: Settings > About phone > tap Build number 7 times.
  2. Open Developer options > Select mock location app > choose Max Fake Location.
  3. Turn on device Location and allow the location permission when the app asks.
  4. Pick a point on the map or enter coordinates, then tap Start location.

Common questions

The app says it is not selected as the mock location app.

Open Developer options, find Select mock location app (on some phones under Debugging), and choose Max Fake Location. The app has a shortcut button to Developer options.

Another app still shows my real location.

Some apps check whether a location is a mock location and ignore it. This is an Android feature and cannot be bypassed. Also make sure Location is on and try turning Wi-Fi scanning off while testing.

Does it change my IP address or network location?

No. Only GPS location is simulated. Your IP address and Wi-Fi or cell-network based location stay the same.

How do I enter coordinates?

Use decimal degrees with a dot, for example -6.175392 and 106.827153. For routes, enter one latitude,longitude pair per line, 2 to 50 lines.

The location stops after a while in the background.

Some phones limit background apps to save battery. Keep the notification visible, and if needed allow the app to run without battery restrictions in your phone settings.

How do I stop the simulation?

Tap Stop in the app or in the ongoing notification. Your phone returns to its real location.

Does the map work offline?

Map areas you have already viewed may load from the on-device cache without a connection. New areas and zoom levels need the internet. Coordinates can always be entered manually.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where required by law, a Privacy choices option appears in the app. You can also reset your advertising ID in Android settings.
