Privacy Policy

Max Fake Location for Android (com.maxq.fakelocation) · Effective November 14, 2026

In short

Max Fake Location has no account and no server of its own. The locations you simulate, your routes and your favorites stay on your device. Map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap. The app shows Google AdMob ads, which collect data as described below.

1. Who we are

Max Fake Location is published by Max-Q Studio. Contact: support@maxqstudio.dev.

2. Data stored on your device

The app saves the following locally in its private storage: the last coordinates you entered or simulated, the simulation status and last error message, your saved favorites (name and coordinates, up to 20), and app settings. Cached map tiles are stored in the app's local storage (up to about 150 MB). None of this is sent to Max-Q Studio. Device backup of app data is turned off. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

3. Your real location

Android requires the location permission before an app can act as the mock location provider. Max Fake Location uses it only for that purpose. The app does not track, store or upload your real location, and does not upload the simulated coordinates either.

4. Permissions

  • Precise and approximate location: required by Android to provide mock locations.
  • Foreground service (location): keeps the simulated position running while you use other apps, with an ongoing notification and a Stop button.
  • Notifications: shows that ongoing notification.
  • Internet and network state: load map tiles and ads.

Max Fake Location does not request contacts, camera, microphone, storage or account access.

5. Map tiles (OpenStreetMap)

The map shows tiles from the OpenStreetMap Foundation's tile servers. Only the tiles visible on screen are downloaded. Like any web request, the tile server receives your device's IP address, the app's identifier and which tiles were requested, which can indicate the map area you are viewing. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner and native ads from Google AdMob.

Google Mobile Ads may collect or process your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), the advertising ID or app set ID, ad interactions, and diagnostic information, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in ads.

7. Consent and privacy choices

Max Fake Location uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for advertising consent where the law requires it. When required, a Privacy choices option appears in the app so you can change your choice later. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

8. Children

Max Fake Location is a developer and testing tool. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Security

Map and ad traffic uses encrypted connections. Max Fake Location stores no personal data on any Max-Q Studio server.

10. Changes

If the app's data practices change, this page will be updated with a new effective date.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy: support@maxqstudio.dev.
