Privacy Policy

Effective November 17, 2026 · Max Background Remover for Android · com.maxq.bgremover

Photo processing

Photos are processed on your device. Max-Q Studio does not receive your photos. The app has no account or cloud photo processing. The included model works offline; optional ML Kit setup downloads its model through Google Play services when you choose Download ML Kit.

Files and permissions

The app reads images selected through Android's system picker. The Android camera app gets temporary access to a private capture file. The app does not request broad gallery access or directly access the camera. Older capture files are cleaned up on the next capture while the current edit is retained.

Saving or exporting also keeps a private editable project containing the original, initial cutout and current revision. Private projects and settings are deleted when you clear app data or uninstall. App backup and device-transfer backup are disabled. Gallery and file exports remain independent. Shared PNG copies are stored temporarily in app cache; copies older than one day are cleaned up the next time you share.

Advertising and consent

Google Mobile Ads serves banner, native and rewarded ads. Google and its partners may process IP addresses, advertising identifiers, device/app information, diagnostics and ad interactions to provide, measure and protect advertising. Selected photos are not provided to the advertising SDK. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google advertising information.

Google UMP checks whether consent or privacy options are required. When available, Ad privacy options in Settings lets you change your choice.

Daily export rules

The app stores a daily successful-output count and whether a rewarded ad has appeared. The first two successful outputs each local calendar day have no rewarded offer. Later, choose Watch ad or Cancel. Cancel abandons that output. If an ad cannot load, export continues and the app can offer an ad on a later output. Once a rewarded ad appears, no more rewarded offers are shown that day. The counter resets on the device's local calendar date.

Support and external links

If you email support, we receive the email address, message and attachments you choose to send. Do not include private photos unless needed for your request. Website and email links open external applications, which follow their own privacy practices.

Questions and updates

Contact support for privacy questions or requests concerning support correspondence. Changes to this policy will be published here with a revised effective date.
