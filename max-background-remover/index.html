Max Background Remover turns a photo of a person, pet or product into a transparent PNG on your Android device.

From photo to PNG

  1. Choose a photo or use the Android camera.
  2. Remove the background and inspect the transparent preview.
  3. Select an area to refine the result, then save to Gallery, export a file or share your PNG.

Keep editing

Saved projects in Images retain the original photo and cutout so you can return to an edit.

Processing on your device

The included background-removal model works offline. Automatic mode can use Google ML Kit when its optional model is available. Processing options and manual ML Kit setup are in Settings.

Check the edges

Hair, fur and complex backgrounds may need refinement. Large photos are limited to 12 megapixels and 4096 pixels per side to manage memory.
