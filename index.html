About

Mobile apps, games, and browser tools.

Max-Q Studio is an independent studio based in Indonesia. We create mobile apps, Android games, and web tools for everyday tasks.

Our products include SaldoSehat: Personal Money Tracker for everyday finances, Pretio: Price Tracker with AI for shopping and price comparisons, Max Cam Scanner for turning paper documents into PDF, Word, and Excel, and Blend In!, a multiplayer hide-and-seek game. With Max Q Tools, you can work with Excel, Word, PDFs, images, print layouts, and barcodes in your browser.
