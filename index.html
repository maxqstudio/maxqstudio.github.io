About

We build apps that are useful, and games that are fun.

Max-Q Studio is an independent studio from Indonesia making mobile apps and games for Android. Our apps help people manage small but important parts of their day, like money and shopping, without confusing menus or unnecessary features.

Our games are made to be played together: easy to pick up, full of small details, and fair. Every product starts small, is improved step by step, and respects your privacy: we collect only what a feature needs and explain it plainly.
