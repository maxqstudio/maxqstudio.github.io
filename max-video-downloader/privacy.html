Privacy Policy

Max Video Download for Android (com.maxq.videodownloader) · Effective November 14, 2026

In short

Max Video Download has no account and no server of its own. Your download history stays on your device. The app is free and shows Google AdMob ads, which collect data as described below.

1. Who we are

Max Video Download is published by Max-Q Studio. Contact: support@maxqstudio.dev.

2. Data stored on your device

The app saves, in its private storage, your theme choice and a list of recent downloads (Android download IDs, file names and the link used, so a failed download can be retried). This data is not sent to Max-Q Studio. Device backup of app data is turned off. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it. Downloaded videos are saved in your Downloads folder and are yours to manage.

3. Links you check or download

When you check a link, your phone contacts the site that hosts it (for direct links), Wikimedia Commons (commons.wikimedia.org) or the Internet Archive (archive.org) to confirm it is a supported video. Those requests come directly from your phone and include your IP address and the app's name and version. The actual download is performed by Android's Download Manager. These sites have their own privacy policies. Max-Q Studio does not receive your links.

4. Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to check links, download videos and load ads.
  • Storage (Android 8 and 9 only): to save into your Downloads folder.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google Mobile Ads, if you haven't opted out.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or account access.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows a banner ad, clearly labelled sponsored cards, and a rewarded video ad that you can choose to watch before each download. Google Mobile Ads may collect or process your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), the advertising ID or app set ID, ad interactions, device and app information and diagnostics, for advertising, measurement, analytics and fraud prevention. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data in ads.

6. Consent and privacy choices

The app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for advertising consent where the law requires it, for example in the EEA, UK and some US states. When required, a Privacy choices entry appears under More so you can change your choice. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.

7. Children

Max Video Download is not directed to children under 13 (or the age of digital consent in your country) and we do not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above. Questions: support@maxqstudio.dev.
