Support

Max Video Downloader for Android (com.maxq.videodownloader)

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email support@maxqstudio.dev. Please include your Android version, phone model and, if it helps, the kind of link you tried (you don't need to share the link itself).

A download failed

The Downloads tab explains why. Common reasons: no internet, not enough storage, or the server stopped the download because the link expired or is private. Tap Retry to try again.

Which links work?

HTTPS links to direct video files (MP4, WebM, MOV, M4V, OGV), Wikimedia Commons file pages and Internet Archive item pages. Links from YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, X, Vimeo and Dailymotion open in their official apps; Max Video Downloader does not extract those videos.

Where are my videos?

In your phone's Downloads folder, named starting with MaxVD_.

Why are there ads?

The app is free and supported by Google AdMob ads. You can change your ad privacy choices under More → Privacy choices when your region requires it.

Remove my data

The app keeps only a local download history on your phone. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage removes it. See the Privacy Policy.
